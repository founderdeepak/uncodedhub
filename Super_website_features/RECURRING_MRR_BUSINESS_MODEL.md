## 1. Why Typical Websites Fail to Keep Clients Paying Recurring Fees

When agencies sell a website, the relationship usually ends after handover because:
1. **The site is static**: Once built, it sits there like a digital PDF brochure.
2. **Zero attribution**: The business owner has no idea if a customer called from the website, a friend's referral, or an Instagram post.
3. **No operational integration**: The website doesn't help them run their daily business.

### The Paradigm Shift: Website as an Operating System (WaaS)
With the **Super Website Features**, the website is no longer just a digital business card. It is their **24/7 Digital Receptionist, Estimator & Calendar Booker**:
- It answers customer questions at 11:00 PM via the Chat Widget.
- It calculates accurate price ranges so clients aren't wasting 45 minutes on calls with low-budget tire-kickers.
- It automatically books in-home surveys with travel buffers and sends reminders to both parties.
- It logs every single conversation into a shared CRM inbox.

If they turn off your monthly subscription, **their lead engine stops working**.
