# The Mixed Signals Decoder
### 100 Psychological Signs They’re Catching Feelings (And Fighting It)
**By Deepak Ravi (@founderdeepak)**

---

## Introduction: Why People Fight Feelings

We live in an era where everyone wants deep emotional intimacy, but almost nobody wants to admit it first. 

Catching feelings triggers vulnerability. For most people, realizing they like someone isn't accompanied by a movie soundtrack—it’s accompanied by anxiety, fear of rejection, loss of control, and defense mechanisms. When someone starts to fall for you, their instinct isn't always to confess; very often, their instinct is to **hide it, resist it, or test the waters**.

This book is your field guide to cutting through mixed signals. Based on observational psychology and behavioral patterns that resonated with over 2 million readers on Threads, here are the **100 genuine signs** that someone is falling for you—even when they are doing everything in their power not to show it.

## Chapter 1: The Micro-Behaviors & Body Language (Signs 1–25)

The body betrays what the mouth refuses to say. When someone likes you, their autonomic nervous system reacts whenever you are near.

1. **The Glance-and-Snap**: They look directly at you across the room, but the second your eyes meet theirs, they jerk their gaze away as if caught doing something illegal.
2. **The Posture Shift**: When you walk into the room, their posture immediately changes—they straighten their spine, adjust their shoulders, or subtly angle their feet towards you.
3. **The Voice Modulation**: Their voice softens when speaking to you compared to the louder, performative tone they use with a group.
4. **Nervous Fidgeting**: Smoothing their hair, adjusting their collar, fidgeting with a ring or bracelet, or tapping their drink only when in your immediate presence.
5. **Proximity Gravity**: In a group setting, they somehow always end up standing or sitting right next to you, even if there were ten other empty spots.
6. **The Throat Clear**: An unconscious swallow or clearing of the throat before they speak to you—a classic sign of adrenaline and sudden nervous excitement.
7. **Mirroring Cues**: If you take a sip of your drink, cross your legs, or lean your chin on your hand, they do the exact same gesture within 10 seconds without realizing it.
8. **The Accidental Touch Linger**: When passing you a pen, a drink, or brushing past in a tight hallway, the physical contact lasts half a second longer than normal.
9. **Pupil Dilation**: Their eyes widen slightly when looking at you, giving their gaze a softer, more focused warmth.
10. **The Quiet Spell**: A normally talkative, confident person suddenly becomes unusually quiet and careful with their words when you sit next to them.
11. **Excessive Laughter**: They laugh at your jokes even when the joke wasn’t particularly funny.
12. **Checking Your Reaction First**: Whenever someone in a group tells a joke, their eyes instinctively dart to **you** first to see if you are laughing.
13. **Unconscious Preening**: Checking their reflection in their phone screen or a window immediately before you approach.
14. **Over-Corrected Casualness**: Leaning back excessively or acting almost *too* relaxed to cover up internal nervousness.
15. **The Sudden Blushing**: A flush across their cheeks, ears, or neck when you give them unexpected one-on-one attention.
16. **Leaning In Deep**: Even in a quiet room, they lean in closer than necessary to hear you speak.
17. **Feet Pointed at You**: The "compass rule"—even if their torso is turned to talk to someone else, their feet remain pointed straight at you.
18. **Unsteady Eye Contact**: Holding intense eye contact for two seconds, looking down at your lips, and then quickly breaking away.
19. **Protecting Your Space**: Subtly positioning themselves to block others from bumping into you in crowded spaces.
20. **Fumbling Objects**: Dropping their keys, misplacing their phone, or spilling a little water when you unexpectedly talk to them.
21. **The Smile They Try to Swallow**: Smiling when they look at you, then immediately pressing their lips together to suppress it.
22. **Touching Their Own Face**: Touching their lips, cheek, or neck while listening to you speak.
23. **Breath Catch**: A noticeable pause in their breathing when you enter their personal space or make unexpected eye contact.
24. **Open Torso Orientation**: Removing barriers between you—moving bags, cups, or laptops aside so there is nothing separating you.
25. **The Reluctant Parting**: When saying goodbye, their feet remain planted, stretching the final 10 seconds into a minute.

## Chapter 2: The Conversational & Texting Signs (Signs 26–50)

When someone likes you and is fighting it, their communication oscillates between intense engagement and artificial hesitation.

26. **The Hyper-Detailed Memory**: They casually bring up something tiny you mentioned three weeks ago (your dog's name, your favorite candy, an offhand comment).
27. **Rapid Follow-Up**: If they have to leave a conversation, they text later explaining why: *"Sorry, had to jump on a call."* (Uninterested people don't explain).
28. **Double-Texting to Clarify Tone**: Sending a second text right away so you don't misunderstand or think they were being dry: *"Haha wait, I didn't mean it like that!"*
29. **The Artificial Delay**: You can tell they were active online, but they wait exactly 15–20 minutes to reply to avoid looking "too eager."
30. **Asking "Why" Questions**: Instead of basic small talk, they probe deeper into your mindset: *"Why did you choose that career?"* or *"What made you want to move here?"*
31. **Sharing Music & Niche Content**: Sending you songs, articles, or memes with: *"This made me think of you."*
32. **Checking If You're Single (Without Asking Directly)**: Sneaking in subtle probes like: *"Did you go with friends or a date?"* or *"Your weekend sounded fun, who were you with?"*
33. **Late-Night Message Windows**: Texting you late in the evening when their mental defenses are down and your name is the last thing on their mind.
34. **Remembering Inside Jokes**: Referencing a small shared joke from days ago to establish a private bond only the two of you share.
35. **The "Accidental" Message**: Sending something trivial or a meme just to open up a conversation thread: *"Look at this, reminded me of what you said."*
36. **Not Letting the Chat Die**: When a topic ends, they ask a new question instead of letting the conversation naturally conclude.
37. **Expressing Concern**: Noticing small shifts in your mood before anyone else: *"You seemed tired today, everything okay?"*
38. **Teasing as a Defense**: Playfully teasing you about small habits. Teasing allows them to flirt while maintaining plausible deniability.
39. **Self-Deprecating Honesty**: Admitting their flaws or quirks to see if you accept the real, unpolished version of them.
40. **Checking In After Social Events**: Texting you after a party or gathering: *"Did you get home safe?"*
41. **Praising What Others Overlook**: Complimenting your thinking, your calmness, or your perspective rather than generic physical traits.
42. **Using Your Name Often**: Sprinkling your name naturally into sentences: *"You know, Deepak, I was thinking about that..."*
43. **Mirroring Your Text Style**: Adopting your emojis, punctuation style, or slang over time.
44. **Sharing Photos of Their Day**: Sending unprompted photos of their view, coffee, or pet—inviting you into their private world.
45. **Remembering Important Dates**: Wishing you luck on an interview, test, or presentation that you only mentioned once in passing.
46. **Defending You When You're Not There**: Speaking up for you in group conversations if someone questions your opinion.
47. **Hesitating Before Sending**: The typing bubbles (`...`) appear, disappear, and reappear multiple times before a text arrives.
48. **Re-Opening Closed Loops**: Responding to an Instagram/Threads story that had no obvious question, just to start talking.
49. **Softening Their Boundary**: Agreeing to things they normally dislike (e.g., trying a food, watching a show) just because you recommended it.
50. **The Morning Touchpoint**: Sending a text early in the day—proof you were their first conscious thought upon waking up.

## Chapter 3: The Psychology of "Fighting the Feeling" (Signs 51–75)

This is where most people get confused. When someone is afraid of catching feelings, their behavior becomes hot-and-cold. Understanding this prevents heartbreak.

51. **The Sudden Pull-Back**: After an amazing, deeply intimate evening or conversation, they go unusually quiet for 24–48 hours to "recalibrate" their emotions.
52. **Mentioning How "Independent" They Are**: Proclaiming how much they enjoy being single or how busy they are with work—a verbal shield against vulnerability.
53. **The Micro-Jealousy Flicker**: When you mention someone else attractive or an ex, their facial expression freezes or their energy drops instantly.
54. **Downplaying Your Connection in Public**: Being warm and flirty in private, but acting strictly platonic or distant in front of mutual friends.
55. **The Sarcastic Deflection**: Deflecting genuine romantic compliments with a joke or sarcasm because real vulnerability overwhelms them.
56. **Excessive Advice Giving**: Trying to position themselves as your "mentor" or "advisor" to stay close to you while avoiding romantic labeling.
57. **Over-Apologizing for Closeness**: Saying things like *"Sorry if I'm bothering you"* or *"I don't want to take up your time,"* testing if you actually want them around.
58. **Reluctant Confessions**: Slipping up and saying: *"I really like talking to you... probably too much."*
59. **Noticing When You Pull Back**: The second you match their distance and stop initiating, they panic and immediately reach out with high energy.
60. **Staring When You Look Away**: You catch them watching you intently when they think you are occupied with someone else.
61. **Asking Mutual Friends About You**: Trying to find out your relationship status or what you think of them through third parties.
62. **The "We Shouldn't" Warning**: Saying phrases like: *"We get along too well,"* or *"You're trouble,"* or *"I need to be careful around you."*
63. **Unprovoked Honesty Spills**: Having late-night emotional disclosures where they tell you secrets they've never told anyone else.
64. **Hyper-Sensitivity to Your Rejection**: Misinterpreting a delayed text or distracted glance as proof that you don't care about them.
65. **Comparing You to Past Hurts**: Saying: *"You're not like the people I usually date,"* signaling that you are breaking their emotional patterns.
66. **Creating Artificial Friction**: Picking small, playful arguments just to create emotional intensity and test if you will stay.
67. **Checking Your Social Activity**: Watching your stories within minutes, but deliberately waiting hours to "like" or reply.
68. **Physical Avoidance After High Intimacy**: Avoiding sitting next to you the day after an intense emotional conversation.
69. **Giving You a Meaningful Nickname**: Using a special pet name or humorous moniker reserved exclusively for you.
70. **Validating Your Worth Constantly**: Reminding you: *"You deserve someone who treats you well"* (often while wishing it were them).
71. **Protectiveness Over Your Safety**: Walking you to your car, checking on your ride, or ensuring you got inside safely.
72. **Refusing to Label What It Is**: Avoiding the "What are we?" talk not because they don't care, but because naming it makes it real and fragile.
73. **Unusually Generous Favors**: Doing substantial favors for you that they wouldn't dream of doing for ordinary acquaintances.
74. **Struggling to Look Away During a Hug**: Hugs that linger with extra firmness, where they exhale deeply before letting go.
75. **The Unspoken Energy**: That unmistakable electric tension in the car or room where both of you know what’s happening, but neither is speaking.

## Chapter 4: The Decisive Clues & Green Lights (Signs 76–100)

When their defenses begin crumbling, these 25 signs reveal they are ready for you to make the move.

76. **They Create Scenarios to Be Alone With You**: Inviting you to run an errand, grab a quick coffee, or study together without a group.
77. **They Openly Share Their Insecurities**: Letting down their armor and telling you about their past failures or fears.
78. **They Introduce You to Their Closest Circle**: Bringing you around their best friend or siblings—the ultimate vetting process.
79. **Their Friends Already Know Everything About You**: When you meet their friends, their friends give you "that look" and already know your backstory.
80. **They Make Time When They Are Truly Busy**: Even during crunch weeks, they find 30 minutes to see you or call you.
81. **They Remember How You Take Your Coffee/Drink**: Ordering your preference without having to ask.
82. **They Initiate Future Plans**: *"We should definitely go there next month"* or *"Let's watch that movie when it comes out."*
83. **They Ask for Your Perspective on Big Decisions**: Valuing your opinion on their career, lifestyle, or family dilemmas.
84. **Physical Barriers Disappear**: No folded arms, no leaning back—they face you fully with open, welcoming body language.
85. **They Compliment Your Character**: Telling you: *"You have such a calming energy,"* or *"I feel like I can be myself around you."*
86. **They Express Jealousy Without Realizing It**: Questioning who you were with, then quickly trying to sound casual: *"Oh, just curious!"*
87. **They Soften When You Touch Them**: If you touch their shoulder or arm, their muscles relax instead of tensing up.
88. **They Match Your Cadence**: Talking slower when you talk slow, adopting your rhythm and energy.
89. **They Give You Their Undivided Attention**: Turning their phone face down on the table when they sit across from you.
90. **They Defend Your Flaws**: Finding your quirky or awkward moments "cute" rather than annoying.
91. **They Bring You Small Gifts**: Grabbing your favorite snack or coffee simply because they passed the store and thought of you.
92. **They Linger at Your Doorstep**: Taking their sweet time unbuckling the seatbelt or walking to the door at the end of a date.
93. **They Drop Hints About Wanting to Be Asked Out**: Saying: *"I really want to try that new restaurant, but none of my friends want to go."*
94. **They Validate Your Availability**: Asking casually: *"So what are you doing this Friday night?"*
95. **They Mirror Your Vulnerability**: When you share something personal, they immediately match it with something equally deep.
96. **They Look at Your Lips**: The classic psychological triangle gaze—eyes, lips, eyes.
97. **They Trust You Around Their Belongings**: Leaving their phone, wallet, or bag with you without a second thought.
98. **Their Demeanor Softens Around You**: Even if they are tough or cynical with the world, they are consistently gentle with you.
99. **They Confess That Time Flies With You**: *"I can’t believe it’s already midnight, where did the time go?"*
100. **The Confession Slip**: Looking at you with genuine fondness and blurting out: *"You really are something special."*

## Action Plan: What to Do Next

If you observed **10+ of these signs**, the attraction is real. If you observed **25+ signs**, they are actively fighting their feelings and waiting for safe permission to express them.

**Your Golden Rule:**  
Do not confront them with heavy emotional pressure (*"Do you like me or what?"*). Instead, **lower the stakes**. Give them a low-pressure invitation where it is emotionally safe for them to step forward.
