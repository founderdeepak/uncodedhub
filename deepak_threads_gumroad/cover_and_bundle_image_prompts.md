## 4. How to Generate in Canva (Free & Easy)
1. In Canva, open a **Book Cover** template (1600 × 2560 px).
2. Set background to Obsidian `#111215` or Slate `#1A1D20`.
3. Add a thin gold border frame (`#D4AF37`) around the canvas with 40px margins.
4. Set Title in **Cinzel Bold** or **Playfair Display** in `#FFFFFF` or `#D4AF37`.
5. Set Author in **Montserrat / Cormorant Garamond**: `DEEPAK RAVI (@founderdeepak)`.
6. Add the signature quote footer at the bottom:
   `"Love dies from neglect long before it dies from conflict."`
7. For 3D mockups: Go to **Apps ➔ Smartmockups ➔ Books / Box Sets**, upload your 2D cover, and Canva will instantly render a realistic 3D book or box set!
