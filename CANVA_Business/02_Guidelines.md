# Uncoded Hub — Brand Guidelines & Design Rules

## 1. Brand Essence & Visual Identity
Uncoded Hub is an ultra-premium, boutique web architecture and high-performance design engineering studio. Our aesthetic is **Swiss Architectural Minimalism** infused with tactile editorial luxury. 

We avoid tacky SaaS gradients, neon cartoonish illustrations, and generic tech clipart. Every design asset must look like it belongs in an international design biennial, an architectural monograph, or an exclusive high-fashion editorial.

---

## 2. The Golden 60/40 Thumbnail Architecture

Every Uncoded Hub article thumbnail follows our signature **60/40 Architectural Split System**:

```
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                                              |
|                     TOP 60% (1600 x 540 px)                  |
|          Tactile Editorial Photography / Model Art           |
|         (Physical raw materials, models, studio art)         |
|                                                              |
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                              |               |
|            BOTTOM 40% - LEFT (70% W)         |  RIGHT (30% W)|
|                                              |               |
|  [Eyebrow / Category Tag] (Uppercase Mono)   |   Custom SVG  |
|  [Article Headline] (Serif / Editorial)      |   Geometric   |
|  [Reading Time / Author / Subtext]           |   Icon Mark   |
|                                              |   + Sub-label |
+--------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Dimensions & Grid:
- **Canvas Size**: `1600 × 900 px` (16:9 aspect ratio, 300 DPI equivalent for crisp Retina rendering).
- **Top Artwork Panel**: `1600 × 540 px` (exactly 60% vertical height).
- **Bottom Split Card**: `1600 × 360 px` (exactly 40% vertical height).
- **Background Fill of Bottom Card**: `#FBFBF9` (Off-white limestone) or `#111111` (Deep matte obsidian for dark mode cards).
- **Vertical Divider Line**: `1 px` solid `#E5E5E0` positioned at `1120 px` from the left edge (70/30 horizontal split).

## 3. Typography Rules in Canva

1. **Title Treatment (The Hook)**:
   - Font: **Cormorant Garamond** (Bold or Semi-Bold) or **Playfair Display**.
   - Size: `56 px – 68 px` depending on line count (maximum 3 lines).
   - Line Spacing: `1.15`.
   - Letter Spacing: `-15` to `-25` (tight editorial tracking).
   - Color: `#111111` (Matte Obsidian). Never pure `#000000`.

2. **Eyebrow / Silo Tag**:
   - Font: **Space Mono** (or **Montserrat** uppercase).
   - Size: `16 px – 18 px`.
   - Letter Spacing: `+120` to `+180` (wide letter tracking for editorial luxury).
   - Color: Accent color assigned to the specific niche (e.g., `#B8860B` for Interior Design, `#0E7490` for Real Estate).

3. **Subtext / Meta Tag**:
   - Font: **Inter** (Regular / Medium).
   - Size: `18 px – 22 px`.
   - Color: `#6B7280` (Neutral stone gray).

---

## 4. Photography & Artwork Guidelines (Top 60%)

1. **Physical & Tactile Realism**:
   - Backgrounds should resemble physical maquettes, raw concrete, fluted glass, polished travertine, brass details, high-end studio lighting, and tangible scale models.
   - **Never** use floating neon 3D icons, thumbs-up avatars, or generic cartoon figures.
2. **Color Palette Matching**:
   - The top artwork must reflect the exact tone assigned to the niche (e.g., warm limestone and bronze for architecture; crisp slate and glass for real estate; pure sterile porcelain for dental).
3. **Framing & Depth of Field**:
   - Macro lens feel with shallow depth of field (f/2.8). Soft, realistic directional sunlight casting subtle architectural shadows.

---

## 5. Right-Panel Geometric Vector Icon Rules

1. **Icon Placement**:
   - Centered inside the right 30% panel (`1120 px` to `1600 px` horizontal span).
   - Size: `120 × 120 px` bounding box.
   - Stroke Width: `1.5 px – 2 px` consistent clean stroke.
2. **Sub-label Beneath Icon**:
   - Monospace font (`Space Mono`), uppercase, `13 px`, tracking `+200`.
   - Examples: `ARCH.STUDIO.01`, `RE.METRIC.V2`, `DENTAL.CLINIC.SPEC`.

---

## 6. Clear DOs and DON'Ts

### ✅ DO:
- Keep the horizontal dividing line razor sharp (1 px).
- Align the left title text with a generous `60 px` left margin.
- Use Canva's **Snap to Grid** and **Position > Align** tools to maintain absolute mathematical precision.
- Export all final thumbnails as **PNG** or **WebP** at 100% quality.

### ❌ DON'T:
- Never place unreadable body paragraphs inside thumbnails.
- Never use more than 2 font families on a single canvas.
- Never place bright gradient drop shadows under text.
- Never crop the primary logo mark or stretch aspect ratios.
- Never use emojis in thumbnail headline graphics.
