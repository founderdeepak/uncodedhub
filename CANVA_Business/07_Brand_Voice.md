# Uncoded Hub — Brand Voice for Canva Business

In **Canva Business**, you can set a permanent **Brand Voice** in **Brand > Brand Kit > Brand Voice**. When using **Magic Write** or generating text across designs, Canva's AI will automatically mimic our exact tone, vocabulary, and intellectual rigor.

---

## 1. Copy-Paste Brand Voice Description for Canva

Copy and paste the exact text below directly into the Canva Brand Voice settings field:

```text
Uncoded Hub is an ultra-premium, boutique web architecture and high-performance design engineering studio. 

Our voice is authoritative, intellectually sharp, deeply disciplined, and editorial—reminiscent of architectural monographs, Swiss international design criticism, and high-end engineering documentation.

Tone Characteristics:
- Confident, precise, and uncompromising on craftsmanship.
- Rejects generic SaaS buzzwords, clickbait phrasing, fluff, and superficial jargon.
- Speaks directly to discerning founders, elite architects, clinical directors, and high-ticket service leaders.
- Treats digital experiences as tangible physical architecture: we talk about foundations, load-bearing code, structural speed, golden ratios, and uncompromising aesthetics.
- Concise and punchy: we let the quality of the work and mathematical performance (100/100 Core Web Vitals) speak for itself.
- Never cheesy, never apologetic, never overly casual. Always sophisticated, calm, and mathematically certain.
```

## 3. Sample Magic Write Prompts for Canva
When asking Canva's Magic Write to rewrite or shorten a blog title for a thumbnail:

- **Prompt**: *"Rewrite this article headline into a punchy, 5 to 7 word high-end architectural headline fitting for a luxury design biennial: '[Paste your raw article title]'."*
- **Example Input**: *"How interior designers can build websites that convert ultra wealthy homeowners into $100k projects"*
- **Example Output**: *"Architecting High-Ticket Client Acquisition for Interior Designers"*
