## 🚀 Step-by-Step Canva Business Brand Kit Setup

### Step 1: Open Canva Brand Kit
1. Sign in to your [Canva](https://www.canva.com) account.
2. In the left navigation menu, click **Brand** (or **Brand Hub**).
3. If you have multiple brands, click **Add new brand kit** and name it:
   `Uncoded Hub — Official Brand Kit`.

---

### Step 2: Upload Logos (`04_Logos/`)
1. Click **Logos > Add new**.
2. Drag and drop all files from [`CANVA_Business/04_Logos/`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Downloads/uncoded-hub/CANVA_Business/04_Logos/):
   - `logo-large.png` (Primary light logo)
   - `logo-small.png` (Compact header mark)
   - `apple-touch-icon.png` (Monogram icon badge)

---

### Step 3: Configure Colors (`05_Colours/`)
1. Under **Colours**, create your primary palette named: `Uncoded Hub — Core`:
   - Primary Dark: `#111111`
   - Primary Light: `#FBFBF9`
   - Accent Flame: `#FF3B00`
   - Neutral Gray: `#6B7280`
   - Subtle Border: `#E5E5E0`
2. Click **Add custom palette** and add the 7 niche palettes from [`05_Colours/Brand_Palettes_CheatSheet.md`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Downloads/uncoded-hub/CANVA_Business/05_Colours/Brand_Palettes_CheatSheet.md):

   - `Real Estate & Builders`: `#0E7490`, `#0F172A`, `#38BDF8`, `#F8FAFC`
   - `Dental Clinics`: `#059669`, `#1E293B`, `#6EE7B7`, `#F0FDF4`
   - `Wedding Photographers`: `#7C3AED`, `#18181B`, `#FDE68A`, `#FAF5FF`
   - `Home Renovation`: `#D97706`, `#27272A`, `#FBBF24`, `#FFFBEB`
   - `Coaches & Consultants`: `#4F46E5`, `#111827`, `#818CF8`, `#EEF2FF`

### Step 4: Configure Fonts (`06_Fonts.md`)
Under **Fonts**, assign your standard brand typography:
- **Title**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Bold)
- **Subtitle**: `Space Mono` (Bold, ALL CAPS)
- **Heading**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Semi-Bold)
- **Body**: `Inter` (Regular)
- **Caption**: `Space Mono` (Regular)

### Step 5: Set Brand Voice (`07_Brand_Voice.md`)

2. Copy the system prompt block from [`07_Brand_Voice.md`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Downloads/uncoded-hub/CANVA_Business/07_Brand_Voice.md) and paste it into Canva.
