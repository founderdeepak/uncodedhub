## 3. How to Input into Canva Brand Kit
1. In Canva, go to **Brand** in the left sidebar.
2. Under **Brand Kit**, select **Colours**.
3. Create your primary palette titled `Uncoded Hub — Core`.
4. Click **Add custom palette** for each of the 7 niches above and paste the HEX values.
