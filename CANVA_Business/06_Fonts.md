# Uncoded Hub — Canva Fonts Setup & Typographic System

Canva Business allows you to define default brand fonts for **Title**, **Subtitle**, and **Body**, plus upload custom `.ttf` or `.woff2` files if desired.

---

## 1. Canva Brand Kit Font Assignments

Configure your font roles in Canva Brand Kit (**Brand > Brand Kit > Fonts**):

| Canva Font Role | Primary Recommended Font | Alternate / Canva Native | Weight | Tracking / Letter Spacing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Title / Heading** | **Cormorant Garamond** | *Playfair Display* / *Bodoni Moda* | Semi-Bold / Bold | `-20` (Tight editorial feel) |
| **Subtitle / Eyebrow** | **Space Mono** | *Anonymous Pro* / *Montserrat* (ALL CAPS) | Regular / Bold | `+140` to `+180` (Wide luxury tracking) |
| **Body / Read Time** | **Inter** | *Helvetica Neue* / *Open Sans* | Regular / Medium | `0` (Normal readability) |
| **Caption / Code Stamp** | **Space Mono** | *PT Mono* / *Roboto Mono* | Regular | `+100` |

## 2. Thumbnail Typographic Hierarchy (1600 × 900 Canvas)

When applying typography in the bottom 40% editorial card (`1600 × 360 px`):

### 🏷️ 1. Eyebrow Category Tag (Top of Bottom-Left Card)
- **Font**: `Space Mono` or `Montserrat` (ALL CAPS)
- **Size**: `16 px`
- **Line Spacing**: `1.0`
- **Letter Spacing**: `+160`
- **Color**: Niche Primary Accent (e.g. `#B8860B` for Interior Design)
- **Position**: Left aligned at `X: 80 px`, `Y: 585 px`

### 📰 2. Article Headline (Main Visual Hook)
- **Font**: `Cormorant Garamond` (Bold)
- **Size**: `56 px – 68 px` (adjust size to fit within 2 to 3 lines maximum)
- **Line Spacing**: `1.15`
- **Letter Spacing**: `-20`
- **Color**: `#111111` (Matte Obsidian)
- **Position**: Left aligned at `X: 80 px`, `Y: 625 px`
- **Max Width**: `960 px` (ensures generous breathing room before the vertical divider at `X: 1120 px`)

### ⏱️ 3. Reading Time & Metadata (Bottom of Bottom-Left Card)
- **Font**: `Inter` (Medium)
- **Size**: `18 px`
- **Line Spacing**: `1.0`
- **Letter Spacing**: `0`
- **Color**: `#6B7280` (Architectural Stone Gray)
- **Format**: `8 MIN READ  •  UNCODED ARCHITECTURE`
- **Position**: Left aligned at `X: 80 px`, `Y: 825 px`

### 📐 4. Right-Panel Geometric Sub-Label (Beneath SVG Icon)
- **Font**: `Space Mono` (ALL CAPS)
- **Size**: `13 px`
- **Line Spacing**: `1.0`
- **Letter Spacing**: `+200`
- **Color**: `#6B7280` or Niche Accent
- **Format**: `ARCH.GOLDEN-RATIO.V1`
- **Position**: Centered under the icon at `X: 1360 px`, `Y: 800 px`

---

## 3. Font Installation in Canva
All three recommended fonts are available natively inside Canva:
- **Cormorant Garamond**: Free Google Font natively available in Canva search.
- **Space Mono**: Free Google Font natively available in Canva search.
- **Inter**: Free Google Font natively available in Canva search.

*Tip: If you prefer an even heavier high-fashion display serif, you can upload **Canela** or **Ogg** as custom fonts via Canva Brand Kit's "Upload a font" button.*
