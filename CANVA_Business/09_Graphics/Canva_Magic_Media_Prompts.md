Use these master prompts in **Canva Magic Media** (or external tools like Midjourney, Google Imagen, or DALL-E) to generate the top 60% artwork (`1600 × 540 px`) for each of the 7 industry niches.

## Universal Aesthetic Formula for Top 60% Artwork
To maintain visual consistency across all 140 articles, every prompt adheres to:
1. **Subject**: Physical scale models, tactile materials, architectural maquettes, studio macro photography.
2. **Lighting**: Soft directional studio light, dramatic natural shadows, architectural lighting.
3. **Texture**: Raw concrete, fluted glass, brushed bronze, polished travertine stone, linen paper, timber joinery.
4. **Composition**: Wide panoramic framing (16:9), clean negative space, zero text in the image.

## Instructions for Canva Placement
1. In Canva, generate the image using the Magic Media tab in `Landscape (16:9)`.
2. Drag and snap the image into the **Top 60% Frame** (`1600 × 540 px`).
3. Double click the image in Canva to reposition the focal point vertically so the most striking textures are highlighted.
