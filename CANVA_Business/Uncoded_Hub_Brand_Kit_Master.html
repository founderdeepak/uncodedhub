Logo Usage Rules in Canva

  • Clear Space: Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the capital letter "U" on all four sides.
  • Background Contrast: Place the primary dark logo only on #FFFFFF, #FBFBF9, or tints lighter than 15% gray.
  • Unacceptable Modifications: Do not rotate the logo, do not alter typography tracking, and do not apply drop shadows or neon glows.
