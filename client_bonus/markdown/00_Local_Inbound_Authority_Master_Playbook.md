## Pillar 01: Google Business Profile (GBP) 3-Pack Ranking Blueprint

### 1.1 Category Hierarchy (The 60% Relevance Lever)
Google’s algorithm matches local search queries based primarily on your **Primary Category**:
- Set your primary category to your core commercial offer (e.g., `Interior Designer`, `Dental Clinic`, `Wedding Photographer`).
- Add 3 to 5 verified secondary categories that capture adjacent search terms.

### 1.2 NAP Precision & Service Areas
Ensure your **Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP)** match your new Uncoded Hub website footer character-for-character. If your business travels to clients, define 10 to 15 specific localities surrounding your hub.

### 1.3 Service Menu Architecture
Fill out every sub-service on your GBP profile with a clear starting price and a keyword-rich 200-word description referencing your locality.

### 1.4 Weekly Visual Rhythm
Upload **2 high-resolution project photos every Friday**. Active visual profiles receive 520% more calls and 1,065% more website visits.

## Pillar 02: Automated 1-Click WhatsApp 5-Star Review Funnel

### 2.1 The Direct 1-Click Review Short-Link
Generate your dedicated Google review short link (`https://g.page/r/.../review`) which opens directly with 5 stars highlighted on mobile screens.

### 2.2 The Peak Emotional Moment
Ask for reviews at the exact moment of client delight: immediately following delivery, handover, or a successful milestone.

### 2.3 The Core WhatsApp Review Request Script
> "Hi [Client Name]! It was such a pleasure working with you on [Project / Service Name] today. Seeing your reaction to the final result made our entire team's week! 
> 
> As an independent studio, honest reviews from clients like you make a huge difference in helping other businesses find us. 
> 
> If you have 45 seconds, could you share a quick review on our Google page? Here is the direct link: [Your Google Review Link]
> 
> Thank you so much for trusting us with your project!"

### 2.4 Keyword-Rich Owner Responses
Respond to every review within 24 hours mentioning the client's name, the specific service delivered, and the city/locality to feed Google's semantic indexing.
