## The 4 Golden Rules of High-Value Inbound Communication

1. **Acknowledge Instantly**: Never leave an inquiry on "Seen" or unattended. If you are in a meeting, have a canned 1-tap template ready.
2. **Never Quote a Naked Price**: Giving a single number without context devalues your expertise and turns you into a commodity. Always frame pricing around scope and outcome.
3. **Control the Next Step**: Every response must end with a clear, low-friction question or booking option. Never end with *"Let me know if you have questions"*.
4. **Communicate with Executive Calm**: Desperation repels high-ticket clients. Communicate with the relaxed, assured certainty of a top-tier specialist.

## 7 Word-for-Word Conversion Scripts

---

### Script 1: The "What is Your Price?" Defense
*The most common inquiry: "Hi, what are your charges / prices for [Service]?"*

**The Strategy**: Acknowledge the question directly, explain the scope variables in 1 sentence, and give a realistic starting range followed by a diagnostic question.

> "Hi [Prospect Name]! Thank you for reaching out to [Business Name]. 
> 
> Our [Service Name] typically starts from [Starting Price, e.g., ₹25,000 / $1,200], with the exact investment depending on [Key Variable 1: e.g., square footage, number of rooms, scope of custom cabinetry].
> 
> To make sure I give you an accurate recommendation without wasting your time, could you tell me: [Key Diagnostic Question, e.g., What is the approximate size of your space, or when are you looking to start]?"

---

### Script 2: The "Send Details on WhatsApp" Busy Prospect
*Inquiry: "Just send details / brochure on WhatsApp."*

**The Strategy**: Provide a concise 3-bullet summary + your curated portfolio link, then invite them for a quick 10-minute discovery call.

> "Hi [Prospect Name]! Here is a quick 3-point overview of how we handle [Service Name]:
> 
> • [Key Deliverable 1, e.g., 100% Custom 3D Architectural Renders & Spatial Planning]  
> • [Key Deliverable 2, e.g., Fixed Milestone Pricing with Zero Hidden Extra Charges]  
> • [Key Deliverable 3, e.g., On-Time Completion Guarantee backed by direct founder oversight]  
> 
> You can explore our recent completed projects here: [Your Website Portfolio URL]
> 
> Would you have 10 minutes tomorrow afternoon for a brief call so we can discuss your specific project needs? I have slots at 2:30 PM and 5:00 PM."

---

### Script 3: The Competitor Price Comparison Objection
*Prospect: "Another provider / agency offered to do this for 40% cheaper. Can you match their price?"*

**The Strategy**: Never demean the competitor. Acknowledge that cheaper alternatives exist, and clearly differentiate why your quality, speed, and reliability protect their investment.

> "I completely understand, [Prospect Name], and there will always be providers who operate at lower upfront rates. 
> 
> The difference with us is in the execution and protection: [Highlight 2 Major Value Pillars: e.g., We use certified 10-mil self-healing TPU with 7-year warranties / We guarantee sub-0.8s mobile speed and 100% clean code with zero recurring plugin fees]. 
> 
> Most of our clients come to us after spending double trying to fix a cut-rate job. We prefer to do it right the first time with guaranteed results. 
> 
> If quality, reliability, and peace of mind are your priority, we’d love to work together. Would you like to review our detailed project scope?"

---

### Script 4: The Frictionless Consultation / Visit Booking
*Prospect: "I’d like to book an appointment / visit your studio."*

**The Strategy**: Give two specific binary time slots rather than an open-ended *"When are you free?"*.

> "Fantastic, [Prospect Name]! We would love to welcome you. 
> 
> We have two consultation slots available this week:
> • Option 1: Thursday at 11:30 AM  
> • Option 2: Friday at 4:00 PM  
> 
> Which of those works better for your schedule? Once you confirm, I will lock in the slot and send our studio location pin and prep notes."

---

### Script 5: The "24-Hour Ghost" Re-Engagement Ping
*When an interested prospect goes completely silent after you send details.*

**The Strategy**: Use the "Polite Permission to Close" framework. It triggers loss aversion without being pushy.

> "Hi [Prospect Name]! Just following up on our conversation from yesterday regarding [Project Name]. 
> 
> We are finalizing our upcoming monthly project schedule this week. Should I keep a tentative slot open for your project, or has your timeline shifted for now? 
> 
> Either way, wishing you all the best!"

---

### Script 6: The "I Need to Check with My Spouse/Partner" Script
*Prospect: "This looks good, but I need to talk to my partner/business co-founder first."*

**The Strategy**: Validate their collaborative process, and equip them with a clean 1-page summary or demo link to show their partner.

> "That makes complete sense, [Prospect Name]—a project like this is an important investment and both partners should feel 100% aligned.
> 
> To make it easy for your partner to review, I’ve put together a quick 1-page summary of the scope and deliverables right here: [Direct Link to Website Project Spec].
> 
> Would it help if we hop on a brief 10-minute joint call together with both of you on Thursday to answer any questions directly?"

---

### Script 7: The Advance Deposit / Commitment Close
*When the client is ready to move forward.*

**The Strategy**: Professional, clear, and reassuring administrative instructions with transparent payment terms.

> "Wonderful, [Prospect Name]! We are thrilled to partner with you on [Project Name]. 
> 
> Here are the next simple steps to secure your sprint slot:
> 
> 1. We have generated your formal Project Scope Sheet and GST Invoice: [Invoice / Document Link].  
> 2. As per our 50/50 milestone policy, the 50% advance deposit confirms your project start date on [Date].  
> 3. You can transfer securely via UPI / NEFT to the bank details listed on the invoice.
> 
> Once received, our founders Deepak & Geetha will initiate Day 1 onboarding and send your dedicated kickoff intake! Looking forward to an extraordinary build."
