01 · Diagnosis

Why it read as AI-generated

The old site used the exact visual vocabulary every AI-built site has shipped since 2024: a #11142A midnight ground, a cyan-to-magenta gradient, glassmorphism panels, blurred colour orbs, rounded-3xl on every surface, and headlines split so the second half was filled with a gradient.

Underneath that, nothing had hierarchy. Every section carried the same py-24. Every card carried the same border and radius. Every heading was the same size and weight. When everything is emphasised nothing is, and the absence of editorial judgment is a stronger tell than any single colour choice.

The copy stacked adjectives — world-class, cutting-edge, premium, ultra-fast, lightning-fast — dozens of times across five pages. Making Websites Win is direct about this: benefits have to be concrete. Adjectives are what you write when you have no evidence.

The structural problem: this site is your product demo. Every weakness in it is a live argument against hiring you, and every claim in the copy is being checked against the page the reader is standing on.

02 · Decisions

Three things were locked before any pixels moved

Both books insist on this order — strategy in words first, design second. These were your calls; everything downstream follows from them.

Positioning

Broad — any business

All coaching-specific titles, Open Graph tags, and JSON-LD have been removed. Every share of every page used to preview as “Coaching Website Design in 7 Days”.

Proof

Fabricated work removed

Four invented case studies and their invented metrics are gone. The Work page now argues from things a prospect can verify.

Direction

Editorial studio

Warm paper ground, one vermillion accent, a serif display set very large against mono micro-labels. Hierarchy from scale and space alone.

03 · Measured

What the rebuild cost the visitor

Three.js, GSAP with ScrollTrigger, Framer Motion, and an icon library were all removed. The Supabase client was moved off the critical path so it only downloads when somebody actually presses Send.

Homepage payload 99.9 kB was 367.0 kB gzipped · −73%
Runtime dependencies 10 was 22 · 236 packages removed
Animation libraries 0 was 3 · now one CSS idiom

The homepage now publishes a performance budget and invites the reader to run Lighthouse against it. That only works as an argument if the numbers hold, which is why the budget was written after the weight came out, not before.

04 · Defects fixed 3 were losing bookings

Bugs that were live in production

Severity Defect Consequence
Critical Booking date built with toISOString() on local midnight In IST that rolls back to 18:30 the previous day. Every booking made from India was saved and sent to Google Calendar dated one day early.
Critical Slots generated in the visitor's local wall clock The page offered “8am–8pm in your timezone” while both hosts are in India. A visitor in New York could book 6:10 AM IST. Availability is now defined once in IST and rendered in the visitor's zone.
Critical base: './' in vite.config with BrowserRouter On any nested route the browser resolved assets to /blog/assets/… — a 404 and a white screen.
High Work page was an orphan Routed and in sitemap.xml but linked from nowhere except one hero button. The page carrying the entire proof argument was effectively unreachable.
High @types/react was never installed; strict mode off TypeScript was checking almost nothing. Both are on now and the codebase is clean under them.
High FAQ imported framer-motion; the project depends on motion Resolved only through a transitive dependency — one clean install from a broken build.
High 15 × focus:outline-none with no replacement Keyboard users could not see where they were. Focus is now a 2px vermillion ring on every control, verified in-browser.
Medium Month paging compared bare month indices The calendar's back button was stuck every January.
Medium FAQ JSON-LD did not match the rendered FAQ Google flags mismatched structured data. Schema is now generated from the same array the page renders.

05 · Removed

Claims that could not survive a question

Three things were saying something the site itself disproved. Each is now either true or gone.

  • The “AI chatbot”. It was a hardcoded three-question script with a fake typing delay and a bot avatar, while “AI chatbot integration” was sold as a headline deliverable. It is now an honestly-labelled enquiry form, and the claim has been removed from the services copy.
  • The four case studies. Apex Dental, Kavya Organics, Vanguard Capital, Velocity SaaS — with metrics like “+140% Monthly Appointments”. Beyond the trust problem, fabricated advertising claims are exposure under India's Consumer Protection Act and the ASCI code.
  • “No-code”. Claimed in the SEO title and two FAQs, while the portfolio badges said React and Next.js and the site itself is hand-built React. A technical buyer catches that in ten seconds.
  • The dummy blog article. /blog/anything rendered a complete fake article — “Using AI within a Workflow”, fabricated byline and date — for any slug typed. The journal now says plainly that nothing is published, carries a noindex, and is out of the nav and sitemap until it is.

The devotional card headed “GOD — The True Founder” has been kept, not cut. Its wording is untouched. It moved from the largest card at the top of the founders grid to a colophon closing the Studio page — a personal statement carries more weight there than it does standing between a prospect and the work.

06 · Open Only you can close these

What is left, and why I stopped short

  1. Put real prices on the Services page

    Every scope has an optional price field — fill it in and the figure renders. Making Websites Win is blunt that vagueness costs conversions, and a services page with no numbers is where studios hide. I left them empty rather than invent figures you would have to walk back on a call.

  2. Publish a privacy policy

    Three forms write names, emails, and phone numbers to Supabase. Under India's DPDP Act — and GDPR for any EU visitor — a policy is required and you do not have one. This needs a lawyer's eye, not mine, so I did not draft text or link to a page that does not exist.

  3. Lock down the Supabase table

    The anon key is in the client bundle, which is normal — but it means anyone can insert rows into contact_submissions directly. I added a honeypot and a minimum time-on-form, which stops naive bots and nothing else. You need row-level security with an insert-only policy, and a rate limit.

  4. Replace the social preview image

    og-image.jpg is a copy of the old coaching artwork, so links still preview with the wrong picture even though the text is fixed. It wants a 1200×630 image in the new identity.

  5. Ship one real project and put it on the Work page

    There is a typed PROJECTS array waiting: add a real client, a real URL, and a number you could defend with a screenshot of the analytics account, and the grid renders itself. This is the single highest-value thing you can do for conversion, and it is the one thing I could not do for you.

  6. Clear the stale build folders from the repo root

    uncoded-hub-build/, uncodedhub_production_build/, uncoded-hub-hostinger/ and several .zip archives are still tracked. One of them breaks Vite's dependency scan on dev start. I left them alone because deleting your archives is your call, not mine.

07 · Identity

The system, so it can be applied without me

Every token lives in src/index.css. Three type voices: a serif that states, a grotesque that explains, a mono that labels and measures. One accent — a vermillion that clears WCAG AA on paper, with a lighter tint of the same hue for dark sections where the base tone only reaches 3.4:1.

The logomark was redrawn. The old one was an eight-axis atom with sixteen nodes and a glow filter; at favicon size it was a grey smudge. The new mark is a solid square with the lower-right quadrant removed — a block deliberately left unclosed — which reads at 16px and has a silhouette you can recognise without the wordmark beside it.

Pages are assembled from shared primitives in src/components/primitives.tsx, so section headers, measure, and rhythm stay consistent. Previously each page invented its own spacing, and that inconsistency was doing real damage.
