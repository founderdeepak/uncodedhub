# The Pre-Sold Prospects Audit

### A 10-point diagnostic to find out how many good prospects your website is silently losing — before you lose another one

## Before you start

This isn't a design checklist. It won't tell you your fonts are wrong or your logo needs work.

It's a **trust diagnostic** — it tells you whether your website actually does the one job that matters: making a serious prospect feel understood and safe enough to reach out, before they ever get on a call with you.

Here's the uncomfortable truth this audit is built on: **the prospects you're losing almost never tell you why.** They don't email you to say "your website didn't convince me." They just go quiet, or they call your competitor instead. The loss never shows up as a number anywhere — it just shows up, eventually, as revenue that never arrived.

A few numbers worth sitting with before you score your own site:

- First impressions of a website form in roughly **50 milliseconds** — and that snap judgment is about 94% design-related.
- **83% of visitors** decide whether a website is credible within 20 seconds, and **87%** have left a site immediately because it didn't look trustworthy.
- **75%** of people abandon a business if its website looks outdated — and **39%** say it makes them reconsider using that business at all.
- **78%** of customers buy from the first business that actually responds to them; roughly half give up if a reply takes more than 6 hours.

*(Sources: Missouri S&T eye-tracking research via CXL/Entrepreneur; HostingAdvice SMB website survey; Blue Fountain Media via Search Engine Journal; LeadResponse/GreetNow response-time benchmarks, 2026. These are US/UK-surveyed figures — the underlying behavior travels, but treat the exact percentages as directional for your market, not a local guarantee.)*

None of this is about traffic. You likely already have enough people looking. This audit is about what happens to them in the first 20 seconds after they land.

**How to use this**: Set aside 30–40 minutes. Open your own website in another tab. Be honest, not hopeful — score what's actually there, not what you meant to put there. Total your score at the end.

## The 10-Point Audit

Score each point **0 (missing), 1 (attempted but weak), or 2 (nailed)**.

### 1. The 5-Second Test
Does your homepage headline name your *exact* ideal client and their *specific* problem — or could it be sitting on any competitor's site with the logo swapped?

*Weak: "Quality service, trusted by many." Strong: names who this is for and what's actually broken in their situation right now.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 2. The Proof-Before-Promise Test
Do you show evidence a skeptical prospect can actually check — a real process, a real guarantee, real work samples, a live demo — or do you mostly just claim to be good?

*Claims are free. Anyone can say "quality craftsmanship." Verifiable specifics are what a burned-before buyer is actually scanning for.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 3. The Objection Pre-Empt Test
Does your site answer, unprompted, the 2–3 objections a serious prospect is silently thinking before they'll message you (price uncertainty, "will they actually deliver," "why you and not the cheaper option")?

*If the only place these get addressed is a live call, you're relying on prospects who message you being brave enough to ask — most just leave instead.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 4. The Single Path Test
Is there one obvious next step on every page — or does a visitor have to figure out whether to call, email, fill a form, or DM you?

*A confused visitor doesn't pick the "best" option. They pick none, and leave.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 5. The Zero-Friction Contact Test
Can a ready prospect reach you in **one click** — WhatsApp, a booking link, a call button — or do they have to find a contact page, fill fields, and wait for a reply?

*90% of consumers use Google Maps/local search before contacting a business, and 60% of mobile users contact a business directly from those results — every extra step between "convinced" and "contacted" bleeds prospects who were already sold.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 6. The Patience Test
Does your site load in **under ~3 seconds** on a normal phone connection — or have you actually timed it?

*Bounce rates jump from roughly 9% to 38% once a page crosses the 3-second mark. A slow site loses prospects before they've read a single word of your pitch.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 7. The Findability Test
If someone is actively searching for exactly what you offer, in your area — can they actually find you (Google Business Profile claimed and current, basic local SEO in place)?

*Being great and being invisible produces the same revenue as being average and easy to find: none, from the people you never reached.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 8. The Risk-Reversal Test
Is there anything on your site that removes the prospect's risk of choosing wrong — a guarantee, transparent terms, a "see it before you commit" option — or are they taking your word for it entirely?

*The less risk a prospect feels, the less convincing you have to do on the call that follows.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 9. The Outcome-Over-Feature Test
Does your copy talk mostly about what you *do* (deliverables, features, process steps) — or about the *outcome* the client's own customers actually feel afterward?

*"12-page website with contact form" is a feature. "Prospects stop disappearing after a call" is an outcome. Prospects buy the second one.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

### 10. The Stranger Test
Hand your homepage to someone who has never met you and give them 20 seconds. Can they say back, unprompted, *who it's for* and *why they'd reach out* — in one sentence?

*If they can't, no amount of traffic fixes that page. This is the single test everything above feeds into.*

**Score: ___ / 2**

## Your 20-Minute Action Checklist

One action per audit point, in the same order. You don't need all ten today — start with whichever one you scored lowest.

- [ ] Rewrite your homepage headline to name your exact ideal client and their specific problem.
- [ ] Add one piece of real, checkable proof above the fold — a guarantee, a real number, a live demo link.
- [ ] Write out your top 2–3 objections and answer them directly on the page.
- [ ] Pick ONE primary action and repeat it on every page — remove competing buttons and links.
- [ ] Add a one-click contact method — WhatsApp or a booking link — not just a contact form.
- [ ] Time your site on a phone, on mobile data. Fix anything over 3 seconds to load.
- [ ] Claim or update your Google Business Profile — current hours, photos, and services.
- [ ] Add a real guarantee or risk-reversal statement near your main call to action.
- [ ] Rewrite your top 3 feature bullets as outcomes your customer feels, not deliverables you provide.
- [ ] Get one honest stranger to read your homepage for 20 seconds and repeat back who it's for.

## What this audit doesn't do

It won't fix your site. It's a diagnosis, not a cure — and being honest about the difference is the whole point. A list of what's wrong is only useful if what comes next is a plan to fix it, not another guess.
