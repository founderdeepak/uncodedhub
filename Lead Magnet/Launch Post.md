# Launch Post — The Pre-Sold Prospects Audit

*Built on the Comment → DM → Delivery skeleton (`uncodedhub-linkedin-system`, lead-magnets-and-email.md §6). Works as a single generic post across all six niches, since the audit itself is universal — no niche-specific rewrite needed for the launch post itself, only for who you tag/target in comments afterward.*

---

## Post copy

I've sat through discovery calls with interior designers, dentists, wedding photographers, and coaches — different industries, same silent leak every single time.

Their prospects don't say "your website didn't convince me." They just go quiet. Or they call the competitor instead. And the business never finds out why — it just shows up, eventually, as a slow month with no obvious cause.

So I turned the exact 10-part diagnosis we run for every paying client — before we design or write a single page — into a self-serve audit anyone can run on their own site in 30 minutes.

**What's inside:**
→ The 10-point trust test we score every client's site against before touching design
→ A scoring band that tells you if you're in "silent loss," "leaking," or "near your ceiling"
→ The exact questions a skeptical prospect is silently asking that most sites never answer
→ Zero fluff — this is the internal checklist, not a marketing teaser for one

It's free. It's the same standard we hold our own client work to.

**Comment "AUDIT" and connect with me** — I'll send it straight to your DMs.

(Can't DM you if we're not connected — that's a LinkedIn thing, not a me thing.)

## Variant hooks (rotate across weeks, same skeleton)

1. **Authority & Transformation opener**: "I've helped [N] businesses find the exact spot their website was quietly losing them clients. Now I'm giving away the audit we use to find it."
2. **Results-Oriented Case Study opener** *(use only once a real, honest client result exists — do not fabricate one)*: "[Client type] found they were scoring 6/20 on this. Here's the audit that showed them why."
3. **Contrarian opener**: "Your website doesn't have a traffic problem. It has a 20-second problem. Here's how to find yours."

## Visual requirement

Every post needs a mobile-optimized preview image (~800×1000px) showing the audit's cover/scoring band table — not a stock photo. Non-negotiable per the source playbook: visuals are what stop the scroll before the hook gets read.

## Evergreen placement

Once live, pin a preview of this audit in the LinkedIn Featured section with a direct "Get the free audit" CTA, and link it from the site's booking page as a lower-commitment first step for visitors not yet ready to book a call.
