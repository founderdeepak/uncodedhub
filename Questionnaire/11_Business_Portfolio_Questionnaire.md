## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Enterprise
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is the legal company name? What is your name and designation (Managing Director / CEO / Partner)?
2. Where is your headquarters and regional offices located? (Full address & GST state registration)
3. How long has the company been in business? (Year of incorporation)
4. What is the founding story and vision of the enterprise?
5. What industry sector and B2B services do you specialize in?
   - [ ] Management consulting & strategy advisory
   - [ ] IT services, software development & cloud engineering
   - [ ] Industrial manufacturing, fabrication & engineering
   - [ ] Commercial facility management & corporate security
   - [ ] Logistics, freight forwarding & supply chain solutions
   - [ ] Financial advisory, statutory audit & tax consulting
   - [ ] Corporate training & executive leadership development
   - [ ] Other: ___________
6. Who is your primary buyer / decision-maker persona?
   - [ ] Enterprise C-Suite (CEOs, CFOs, CTOs, COOs)
   - [ ] VP / Director of Procurement & Vendor Management
   - [ ] HR Directors / Learning & Development Heads
   - [ ] Factory / Plant Operations Managers
   - [ ] SME & Mid-market business owners
   - [ ] Other: ___________
7. What is your typical contract or project deal size? (e.g., ₹2 Lakh retainer to ₹50 Lakh+ multi-year enterprise contracts)
8. How many corporate clients or projects have you serviced to date?
9. How many full-time staff, engineers, or consultants work in your organization?

26. What do enterprise procurement heads or CXOs say about working with your team? (Verbatim quotes)
