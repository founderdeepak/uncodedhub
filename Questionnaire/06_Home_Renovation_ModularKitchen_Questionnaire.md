## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is your full name and business/studio name?
2. Where are you based? Which cities or areas do you serve?
3. How long have you been in this business?
4. How did you get started — what is your background?
5. What is your primary offering?
   - [ ] Full home renovation (end-to-end)
   - [ ] Modular kitchen design and installation
   - [ ] Custom / modular furniture
   - [ ] Bathroom renovation
   - [ ] Flooring and tiling work
   - [ ] False ceiling and electrical work
   - [ ] Civil + interiors combined
   - [ ] Other: ___________
6. Who is your typical client?
   - [ ] Homeowners doing a fresh renovation (new home)
   - [ ] Homeowners upgrading an old kitchen / bathroom
   - [ ] Newly married couples setting up a home
   - [ ] People doing a resale flat renovation
   - [ ] Builders doing renovation before sale
   - [ ] Commercial kitchen / restaurant owners
   - [ ] Other: ___________
7. What is the average project budget range? (e.g., ₹2L–₹5L for modular kitchen, ₹10L+ for full home renovation)
8. How many projects have you completed? (approximate total)
9. Do you work solo or do you have a team? (In-house carpenters? Site supervisors? Outsourced vendors?)

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What makes YOUR business different from every other renovation contractor or modular kitchen studio in your area?

21. Do you handle permits, approvals, or government clearances if required?
22. Do you have in-house carpenters / workers, or do you outsource? Why does that matter for quality?
23. What is your most impressive completed project? Tell the full story.
24. List your TOP 5 completed projects:
    - Type of project (kitchen / full renovation / furniture)
    - Location (area/city)
    - Year completed
    - Budget range
    - Photos available? (YES / NO)
25. Do you have any certifications, brand partnerships, or manufacturer authorisations?
    *(Authorized dealer for Hettich / Blum / Hafele? Certified installer for brand?)*
26. Have you been featured in any media, design blogs, or local news?
27. What do clients say most often after the project is complete? (Actual quotes)
28. Do you have Google reviews, testimonials, or before-after photos from past clients?
