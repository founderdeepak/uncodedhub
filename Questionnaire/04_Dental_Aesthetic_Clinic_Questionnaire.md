# 🦷 Dental & Aesthetic Clinic — Client Discovery Questionnaire
### Uncoded Hub | Website + Customer Saga + Google Business + Brochure
*75 Questions | Map every answer directly to the Customer Saga Workbook Sections 1–10*

---

## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is the clinic's full name? What is your name (doctor / owner)?
2. Where is the clinic located? (Full address — feeds Google Business)
3. How long has the clinic been operating?
4. How did you start this clinic? What is your background and training?
5. What type of clinic is this?
   - [ ] General and family dentistry
   - [ ] Cosmetic / smile design dentistry
   - [ ] Dental implants specialty
   - [ ] Orthodontics (braces, Invisalign)
   - [ ] Aesthetic / cosmetic skin clinic
   - [ ] Hair loss / PRP clinic
   - [ ] Multi-specialty dental + aesthetic clinic
   - [ ] Other: ___________
6. Who is your typical patient?
   - [ ] Families and children (general dental care)
   - [ ] Young adults (cosmetic improvements — smile, skin)
   - [ ] Working professionals (convenience and premium care)
   - [ ] Patients referred by other doctors
   - [ ] Patients coming for a second opinion
   - [ ] Corporate tie-ups / group health plans
   - [ ] Other: ___________
7. What is the average treatment value? (e.g., ₹8K–₹15K for braces consultation, ₹50K+ for full smile makeover)
8. How many patients do you see per month?
9. How many doctors, dentists, or specialists work at the clinic?

---

## 💛 PART 2 — What Clients Want
*(→ Customer Saga Section 2: Desires)*

10. What do patients ultimately want beyond "fixing a problem"?
    *(Confidence? Appearing younger? Being pain-free? A smile they're proud to show?)*
11. What emotion do patients want to feel after their treatment is complete?
12. What is the transformation your best patients experience — not just physical, but emotional?

---

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

13. What are the most common complaints patients have about OTHER clinics they've visited?
    *(Rushed appointments? Upselling? Painful procedures? Long waiting times? No follow-up?)*
14. What mistakes do bad clinics or doctors make that you would never make?
15. Have you ever treated a patient who had a bad experience elsewhere and came to you to fix it? What happened?
16. What is the most anxious or stressful part of the experience for a new patient?
17. What do patients NOT know before coming in — things that surprise them (positively or negatively)?

---

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What makes YOUR clinic different from the dozens of other dental or aesthetic clinics in your area?
19. What are your qualifications and specialisations? List all degrees, training, and certifications.
    *(BDS, MDS, fellowship, international training, brand certifications like Invisalign Gold Provider?)*
20. What equipment or technology do you use that other clinics in your area don't?
    *(CEREC, digital X-rays, 3D scanner, laser dentistry, advanced PRP kits?)*
21. Do you offer any guarantees or warranties on treatments?
22. What is your most impressive patient transformation? Tell the full story.
    *(Before / after — what was the starting point, what did you do, what was the result?)*
23. List your TOP 5 case types or specialised treatments with best outcomes:
    - Treatment type
    - Patient background (brief)
    - Result / outcome
    - Photos available? (YES / NO — with patient consent)
24. Do you have any awards, recognition, or rankings? (Best clinic awards, media features, doctor of the year?)
25. Have you been featured in newspapers, health magazines, or on TV? (Links or names)
26. What do your happiest patients say after their treatment? (Actual quotes)
27. Do you have Google reviews, Practo reviews, or written testimonials?
28. How many 5-star reviews do you currently have across platforms?

---

## 📣 PART 5 — Call to Action
*(→ Customer Saga Section 5: CTA)*

29. How should new patients book? (Call / WhatsApp / Online booking / Walk-in)
30. Do you offer free consultations? (For all treatments or specific ones?)
31. What information does a patient need to share before booking? (Concern / area / urgency?)

## ⚙️ PART 6 — Your Process
*(→ Customer Saga Section 6: 3-Step Process)*

32. Walk me through the patient journey — step by step:
    1. First contact (call / online booking) →
    2. Initial consultation →
    3. Diagnosis and treatment plan presentation →
    4. Treatment begins →
    5. Progress check-ins →
    6. Completion →
    7. Follow-up / aftercare →
33. What is the typical treatment timeline for your most popular procedures?
34. How do you make anxious patients feel safe and at ease?
35. What is your fee structure? (Transparent upfront quotes? EMI options? Insurance tie-ups?)

## ✨ PART 7 — Features & Benefits
*(→ Customer Saga Section 7: Feature Table)*

36. List everything included in your patient experience:
    - [ ] Free initial consultation
    - [ ] Detailed digital diagnosis / reports
    - [ ] Personalised treatment plan with cost breakdown
    - [ ] Painless / sedation options available
    - [ ] EMI / payment plan options
    - [ ] Same-day / emergency appointments
    - [ ] WhatsApp follow-up after treatment
    - [ ] Treatment warranty / guarantee
    - [ ] Multilingual staff
    - [ ] Parking / accessibility features
    - [ ] Other: ___________
37. What does a patient get from you that they wouldn't get at any other clinic nearby?
38. What small details in your clinic experience leave a lasting impression on patients?
    *(Waiting room comfort? Complimentary drinks? Explain-as-you-go communication style?)*

## 🙅 PART 8 — Objections & Testimonials
*(→ Customer Saga Section 8: Objections)*

39. What are the most common reasons people delay or hesitate before booking?
    *(Fear of pain? Cost? "I'll wait until it hurts more"? Unsure which clinic to trust?)*
40. What do you say to someone who says "Your prices are higher than the clinic near my house"?
41. What do you say to someone who is terrified of dental procedures?
42. What do you say to someone who asks "Will this treatment actually work for me?"
43. What do you say to someone who has had a painful or bad experience at another clinic?
44. Do you have before-after photos (with consent) or patient testimonial videos?

---

## ⚠️ PART 9 — Stakes & Consequences
*(→ Customer Saga Section 9: Stakes)*

45. What happens when people keep delaying dental or aesthetic treatment?
    *(Small problem becomes expensive? Permanent damage? More invasive solution needed later?)*
46. What is the real personal cost of neglecting dental or aesthetic concerns?
    *(Social confidence? Job interviews? Relationships?)*
47. What does life look like for a patient who completes the full treatment with you?
