## ⚙️ PART 6 — The Outpatient Patient Journey
*(→ Customer Saga Section 6: 3-Step Process)*

    3. Dedicated, unhurried consultation with senior specialist doctor →
    4. Immediate on-site diagnostic testing or pharmacy dispensing (if needed) →
    5. Clear explanation of diagnosis, dietary lifestyle guidance, and dosage →
    6. Automated digital prescription sent to patient's WhatsApp →
    7. Post-consultation follow-up check & review booking →
