   - [ ] Deity idols / murthis

   - [ ] Murals and paintings
   - [ ] Standalone sculptures (public, private)
   - [ ] Heritage restoration work
   - [ ] Other: ****\_\_\_****
6. Who typically hires you?
   - [ ] Temple trusts / committees
   - [ ] Government / archaeological bodies

   - [ ] Other: ****\_\_\_****

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem

_(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)_

13. What are the biggest complaints your clients have about OTHER sculptors they have hired before?
14. What mistakes do bad sculptors make that you would never make?
15. Have you ever taken over a project that another sculptor ruined or abandoned? What happened?
16. What is the most frustrating part of the process for a client commissioning a sculpture?
17. What do clients NOT know before they start — things that surprise or stress them mid-project?

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority

_(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)_

18. What makes YOUR work different from other sculptors? Be honest and specific.
19. What scripture or system do you follow for proportions and design? (Manasara? Mayamata? Agama Shastra? Tamil Silpa tradition?)
20. What types of stone and materials do you use and why? (Granite? Marble? Sandstone? Where sourced?)
21. Do you specialize in a particular style or regional tradition? (Dravidian / Chola / Hoysala / Rajasthani / Orissan?)
22. What is the most technically difficult thing you can do that most sculptors cannot?
23. What is your proudest project to date — tell the full story.
24. List your TOP 5 completed projects:
    - Name/type of project
    - Location (city/state)
    - Year completed
    - Size / scale
    - Photos available? (YES / NO)
25. Do you have any awards, recognition, press coverage, or government honours?
26. Have any of your works appeared in newspapers, TV, or social media? (Links or names)
27. What do clients say most often when they see your finished work? (Actual quotes)
28. Do you have any letters of recommendation, certificates, or written praise from clients?
