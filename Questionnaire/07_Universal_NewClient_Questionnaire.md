# 🧩 New Client / Any Niche — Universal Discovery Questionnaire
### Uncoded Hub | Website + Customer Saga + Google Business + Brochure
*75 Questions | Use this for any client that doesn't fit the 6 core niches*
*Map every answer directly to the Customer Saga Workbook Sections 1–10*

---

> **When to use this:** Use this questionnaire for any new client whose business type is not yet covered by the niche-specific questionnaires. After completing this, review which niche template is closest and supplement with niche-specific questions.

---

## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is your full name and business name?
2. Where are you based? Which areas or cities do you operate in?
3. How long have you been running this business?
4. How did you get started — what is your origin story?
5. What product or service do you sell? Describe it in plain language.
6. Who is your typical customer? (Age, profession, location, life stage, spending power)
7. What is the average sale value per client / project? (₹X per transaction)
8. How many clients have you served to date? (approximate)
9. Do you work solo or do you have a team? (How many people, what roles?)

---

## 💛 PART 2 — What Clients Want
*(→ Customer Saga Section 2: Desires)*

10. What does your ideal client ultimately want beyond your product or service itself?
    *(A result, a feeling, a status, a relief from a problem?)*
11. What emotion do your clients want to feel after they've worked with you or used your product?
12. What does the "dream outcome" look like for your best client?

---

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

13. What are the most common complaints your clients have about your competitors?
14. What mistakes do others in your industry make that you would never make?
15. Have you ever had to rescue a client from a bad experience with a competitor? What happened?
16. What is the most frustrating or stressful part of the buying process for your clients?
17. What do clients NOT know before they hire you — things that typically surprise them?

---

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What makes your business different from everyone else doing the same thing in your market?
    *(Be specific — not just "better quality" or "great service")*
19. What qualifications, certifications, or training do you have that others in your field don't?
20. Do you have a proprietary process, method, or system that is uniquely yours?
21. Do you offer any guarantees or risk-reversal for clients?
22. What is your most impressive project, case, or client outcome? Tell the full story.
23. List your TOP 5 projects / clients / outcomes:
    - Description
    - Industry / client type
    - Year
    - Result / outcome
    - Visual proof available? (YES / NO)
24. Do you have any awards, media features, speaking engagements, or industry recognition?
25. Have you been featured in press, podcasts, or online publications? (Links or names)
26. What do your happiest clients say about you? (Actual quotes)
27. Do you have written testimonials, Google reviews, or any verifiable social proof?
28. How many repeat or referred clients have you served?

---

## 📣 PART 5 — Call to Action
*(→ Customer Saga Section 5: CTA)*

29. How should new clients first get in touch? (Call / WhatsApp / Email / Book online / Walk-in)
30. Do you offer a free consultation, discovery call, or initial assessment?
31. What information does a client need to share before you can give them a quote or recommendation?

---

## ⚙️ PART 6 — Your Process
*(→ Customer Saga Section 6: 3-Step Process)*

32. Walk me through your full client journey — step by step:
    1. First enquiry →
    2. Discovery / assessment →
    3. Proposal or plan →
    4. Agreement and payment →
    5. Delivery / service begins →
    6. Progress and updates →
    7. Completion and handover →
33. How long does the average engagement take from start to finish?
34. How do you keep clients updated throughout your process?
35. What is your pricing / payment structure?

---

## ✨ PART 7 — Features & Benefits
*(→ Customer Saga Section 7: Feature Table)*

36. List everything that is included in what you offer:
    *(Think about what clients assume is extra, but you actually include — those are your hidden differentiators)*
    - Feature 1: ___________
    - Feature 2: ___________
    - Feature 3: ___________
    - Feature 4: ___________
    - Feature 5: ___________
    - Feature 6: ___________
    - Feature 7: ___________
    - Feature 8: ___________
    - Feature 9: ___________
    - Feature 10: ___________
37. What do you include in your service that most competitors at your price point don't?
38. What small thing do you do that clients are always pleasantly surprised by?

---

## 🙅 PART 8 — Objections & Testimonials
*(→ Customer Saga Section 8: Objections)*

39. What are the most common reasons people hesitate or delay before hiring you?
40. What do you say when someone says "You're more expensive than someone else I found"?
41. What do you say when someone asks "How do I know you'll actually deliver what you promised"?
42. What do you say when someone asks "How long will it take?"
43. What do you say to someone who has been let down by a similar service provider before?
44. Do you have testimonials, case studies, or reviews you can share?

---

## ⚠️ PART 9 — Stakes & Consequences
*(→ Customer Saga Section 9: Stakes)*

45. What are the worst things that can happen when someone makes the wrong choice in your industry?
    *(Lost money? Wasted time? Problem still unsolved? Worse outcome than before?)*
46. What is the deeper personal or professional cost of a bad decision in your category?
47. What is the best-case scenario for a client who chooses you and you deliver perfectly?

---

## 🔍 PART 10 — Research & Real Language
*(→ Customer Saga Section 10: Additional Research)*

48. What exact words or phrases do potential clients use when they first reach out to you?
49. What questions do clients always ask before saying yes to working with you?
50. What industry trends or changes are affecting your clients' buying decisions right now?
51. Who are your main competitors? What do they do well? Where are they weak?
52. Are you active on social media? (Platform handles / links)

---

## 📸 PART 11 — Visual Assets

53. Do you have photos or visuals of your work, product, or results?
    - [ ] Yes — professional quality
    - [ ] Yes — phone quality
    - [ ] A few
    - [ ] None currently
54. Do you have any videos — process, results, testimonials, or walkthroughs?
55. Do you have a photo of yourself or your team?
56. Do you have any before-and-after visuals or case study evidence?
57. What look and feel do you want the website to have?
    *(Professional? Warm? Premium? Energetic? Trustworthy? Bold? Minimal?)*

---

## 💼 PART 12 — Business Numbers
*(These become credibility stats on the website)*

58. Total number of clients served?
59. Total number of years in business?
60. Number of team members?
61. Total revenue range / volume served? (e.g., "₹1Cr worth of projects delivered")
62. Highest-value single client or project?
63. Number of cities / markets served?
64. Any impressive numbers specific to your industry? (e.g., "500+ deliveries," "10,000+ products sold")

---

## 🌐 PART 13 — Google Business & Local Presence

65. Do you currently have a Google Business Profile? (Yes / No)
66. How many Google reviews do you have?
67. What is your primary service area or target market?
68. What specific search terms do you want to rank for?
    *(Write the exact phrase a customer would type into Google to find you)*

---

## 📋 FINAL — Quick Logistics

69. Phone number and WhatsApp number for the website?
70. Professional email address? (Or do we create one?)
71. Do you own a domain name already?
72. Do you have a logo? (Or do we design one?)
73. When do you want the website live?
74. Are there any websites (competitors or inspiration) you admire? (Send links)
75. Is there anything else about your business, your journey, or your vision for the website that we haven't covered?
