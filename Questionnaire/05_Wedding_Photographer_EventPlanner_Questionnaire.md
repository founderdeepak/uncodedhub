# 💍 Wedding Photographer, Videographer & Event Planner — Client Discovery Questionnaire
### Uncoded Hub | Website + Customer Saga + Google Business + Brochure
*75 Questions | Map every answer directly to the Customer Saga Workbook Sections 1–10*

---

## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is your full name and studio/business name?
2. Where are you based? Which cities and destinations do you cover?
3. How long have you been in this profession?
4. How did you get started? What is your background?
5. What is your primary service?
   - [ ] Wedding photography only
   - [ ] Wedding videography / films only
   - [ ] Photography + videography (both)
   - [ ] Pre-wedding / engagement shoots
   - [ ] Wedding planning and coordination
   - [ ] Destination wedding specialist
   - [ ] Corporate events / brand shoots
   - [ ] Baby / maternity / family photography
   - [ ] Other: ___________
6. Who is your typical client?
   - [ ] Couples planning a traditional wedding
   - [ ] Couples wanting a cinematic / editorial style
   - [ ] Destination wedding couples (outstation)
   - [ ] NRI couples planning weddings in India
   - [ ] Families wanting full event documentation
   - [ ] Brides who are the primary decision-maker
   - [ ] Other: ___________
7. What is your average booking value? (e.g., ₹60K–₹1.5L for photography package, ₹3L+ for destination)
8. How many weddings / events have you covered to date?
9. Do you work solo or with a team? (Second shooter? Videographer partner? Editing team?)

---

## 💛 PART 2 — What Clients Want
*(→ Customer Saga Section 2: Desires)*

10. What does a couple ultimately want beyond "good photos"?
    *(Memories that feel real? Their grandchildren to see them? A film that makes them cry?)*
11. What emotion do clients want to feel when they watch their wedding film or flip through their album?
12. What does the dream outcome look like for your ideal couple?

---

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

13. What are the most common complaints couples have about photographers or planners they've hired before?
    *(Missed moments? Long delivery times? Not the same person who shot the trial shoot? Edited too heavily?)*
14. What mistakes do bad photographers or planners make that you never make?
15. Have you ever heard a horror story from a couple about a previous vendor? What happened?
16. What is the most stressful part of wedding photography or planning for the couple?
17. What do couples NOT know before booking a photographer or planner — things that shock them later?

---

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What is your shooting style signature? How would you describe your work in 3 words?
    *(Documentary / Candid / Editorial / Dark & Moody / Bright & Airy / Cinematic / Traditional?)*
19. What is the gear you use? (Camera bodies, lenses, lighting — this matters to serious couples)
20. Do you edit every photo yourself or outsource editing? How long does delivery take?
21. Do you guarantee that YOU personally will be shooting — not a substitute?
22. What is your most memorable wedding or shoot? Tell the full story.
    *(What made it special? What challenge did you overcome? What was the emotional moment?)*
23. List your TOP 5 projects:
    - Wedding / event type
    - Location (city / venue)
    - Year
    - Style / highlights
    - Can you share the gallery link or samples? (YES / NO)
24. Do you have any awards, features, or recognition in the industry?
    *(WeddingSutra, WedMeGood, Instagram featured, photography awards?)*
25. Have your photos appeared in wedding blogs, magazines, or been viral on social media?
    *(Links or names)*
26. What do your couples say most often after receiving their photos or film? (Actual quotes)
27. Do you have written testimonials, Google reviews, Instagram DMs, or video reviews from couples?
28. What is your average delivery timeline — and how does it compare to industry standard?

---

## 📣 PART 5 — Call to Action
*(→ Customer Saga Section 5: CTA)*

29. How should new couples first reach you? (WhatsApp / Call / Email / Enquiry form / Instagram DM)
30. Do you offer a free consultation or discovery call before booking?
31. What information does a couple need to share before you can check availability?

---

## ⚙️ PART 6 — Your Process
*(→ Customer Saga Section 6: 3-Step Process)*

32. Walk me through your full process — step by step:
    1. First enquiry →
    2. Discovery call / in-person meeting →
    3. Package selection and contract signing →
    4. Advance payment →
    5. Pre-wedding shoot (if applicable) →
    6. Wedding day coverage →
    7. Editing and delivery →
33. How long does delivery take after the event? (Photos? Film?)
34. How many hours of coverage is included in your standard package?
35. What is your pricing structure? (Packages / custom quotes / travel charges?)

---

## ✨ PART 7 — Features & Benefits
*(→ Customer Saga Section 7: Feature Table)*

36. List everything included in your service:
    - [ ] Pre-wedding / engagement shoot
    - [ ] Full-day wedding coverage (specify hours)
    - [ ] Second shooter / second camera
    - [ ] Drone / aerial shots
    - [ ] Candid + traditional coverage
    - [ ] Same-day edit / sneak peek reels
    - [ ] Cinematic wedding film
    - [ ] Online gallery delivery
    - [ ] Printed album (design and print)
    - [ ] Rights to all images / film
    - [ ] Other: ___________
37. What do you provide that most photographers or planners in your price range don't?
38. What part of your service do clients consistently call out as "we didn't expect this but loved it"?

---

## 🙅 PART 8 — Objections & Testimonials
*(→ Customer Saga Section 8: Objections)*

39. What are the most common reasons couples hesitate before booking you?
    *(Budget? Not sure about style fit? Comparing multiple vendors? Parents influencing the decision?)*
40. What do you say when someone says "Your pricing is higher than other photographers I've spoken to"?
41. What do you say when someone asks "Will you personally be shooting our wedding?"
42. What do you say to someone worried about how long delivery will take?
43. What do you say to a couple who is deciding between you and one other photographer?
44. Do you have a portfolio gallery link, Instagram highlights, or video testimonials to share?

---

## ⚠️ PART 9 — Stakes & Consequences
*(→ Customer Saga Section 9: Stakes)*

45. What is the worst thing that can happen when a couple chooses the wrong photographer or planner?
    *(Missed first look moment? Unusable photos from a wedding that can never be re-done? Planner gone AWOL on the day?)*
46. What is the deeper, long-term cost of bad wedding photography beyond the event itself?
    *(Looking at blurry or over-edited photos for the rest of your life? No real documentation of the most important day?)*
47. What is the best-case scenario when a couple chooses you and everything goes right?

---

## 🔍 PART 10 — Research & Real Language
*(→ Customer Saga Section 10: Additional Research)*

48. What exact phrases do couples use when they first approach you?
    *(e.g., "We want it to feel real, not posed," "We want a film like a Bollywood movie," "Our budget is X — what can you offer?")*
49. What questions do couples always ask before committing to a booking?
50. What are the most popular trends couples are requesting right now?
    *(Pre-wedding shoots in Rajasthan? Drone shots? Instagram reels of the wedding? Same-day edits?)*
51. Who are your main competitors in your city? What do they do well? Where are they weak?
52. Are you active on social media? (Instagram / YouTube / Pinterest — handles/links)
