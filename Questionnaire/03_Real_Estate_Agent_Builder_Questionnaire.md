# 🏡 Real Estate Agent & Builder — Client Discovery Questionnaire
### Uncoded Hub | Website + Customer Saga + Google Business + Brochure
*75 Questions | Map every answer directly to the Customer Saga Workbook Sections 1–10*

---

## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is your full name and business/agency name?
2. Where are you based? Which areas, localities, or cities do you operate in?
3. How long have you been in real estate / construction?
4. How did you get started? What is your background?
5. What is your primary business type?
   - [ ] Independent real estate agent / broker
   - [ ] Real estate agency (team of agents)
   - [ ] Small builder / developer (residential projects)
   - [ ] Plot / land consultant
   - [ ] Commercial property consultant
   - [ ] NRI property services
   - [ ] Rental property management
   - [ ] Other: ___________
6. Who is your typical client?
   - [ ] First-time home buyers (end users)
   - [ ] Investors buying for rental income
   - [ ] NRI buyers
   - [ ] People upgrading / downsizing
   - [ ] Commercial buyers (office, retail)
   - [ ] Builders and developers (B2B)
   - [ ] Other: ___________
7. What is the typical transaction value range? (e.g., ₹40L–₹1Cr residential, ₹2Cr+ luxury)
8. How many deals / properties have you closed or projects completed?
9. Do you work solo or do you have a team? (How many agents / support staff?)

---

## 💛 PART 2 — What Clients Want
*(→ Customer Saga Section 2: Desires)*

10. What does a client really want beyond "finding a property"?
    *(Peace of mind? A trusted guide? No hidden surprises? The right deal at the right price?)*
11. What emotion do clients want to feel when they close their purchase successfully?
12. What does the ideal outcome look like for your best-fit client?

---

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

13. What are the biggest frustrations buyers or sellers have with other agents?
    *(Only showing what gives the highest commission? Not available? Poor follow-through?)*
14. What mistakes do bad agents or builders make that you would never make?
15. Have you ever helped a client recover from a bad deal or a bad agent's advice? What happened?
16. What is the most stressful part of the property buying/selling process for clients?
17. What do buyers or sellers NOT know before they start — things that catch them off guard?

---

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What makes you different from every other agent or builder in your area?
19. Do you specialize in a specific locality, project type, or client segment?
20. What specific market knowledge do you have that other agents don't?
    *(Which roads are appreciating? Which builders to avoid? Which projects have legal clarity?)*
21. Do you offer any guarantees or commitments that other agents don't?
22. What is your most successful deal or project? Tell the full story.
23. List your TOP 5 projects / deals / developments:
    - Type of property
    - Location (area/city)
    - Year closed / completed
    - Transaction value range
    - Photos or video available? (YES / NO)
24. Do you have any industry certifications, RERA registrations, or licences?
25. Have you been featured in local media, real estate portals, or news?
    *(Links or names)*
26. What do your happiest clients say about working with you? (Actual quotes)
27. Do you have written testimonials, Google reviews, or referrals from past clients?
28. How many repeat clients or referral-based clients have you served?

## 📣 PART 5 — Call to Action
*(→ Customer Saga Section 5: CTA)*

29. How should interested clients first reach you? (Call / WhatsApp / Fill a form / Schedule a site visit)
30. What is the first thing you do when a new buyer or seller contacts you?
31. What information do you need before you can help them seriously?

## ⚙️ PART 6 — Your Process
*(→ Customer Saga Section 6: 3-Step Process)*

32. Walk me through your full process — step by step for a buyer:
    1. First enquiry →
    2. Needs assessment call →
    3. Property shortlisting →
    4. Site visits →
    5. Negotiation →
    6. Documentation / legal check →
    7. Registration / handover →
33. How long does a typical buying or selling transaction take from start to close?
34. How do you keep clients informed throughout the process?
35. What is your commission structure or fee model?

## ✨ PART 7 — Features & Benefits
*(→ Customer Saga Section 7: Feature Table)*

36. List everything included in your service:
    - [ ] Free initial consultation and needs assessment
    - [ ] Curated shortlist (only properties matching their criteria)
    - [ ] Legal and document verification support
    - [ ] Bank loan / home loan assistance
    - [ ] Negotiation on the client's behalf
    - [ ] Full paperwork and registration support
    - [ ] Post-purchase support (interiors referrals, utility connections, etc.)
    - [ ] Market valuation and investment advice
    - [ ] NRI-specific paperwork and remote purchase support
    - [ ] Other: ___________
37. What do you do for clients that other agents simply don't bother with?
38. Do you use any tools or technology that improve the client experience?
    *(Virtual tours? GIS / location mapping? Portfolio management platform?)*

---

## 🙅 PART 8 — Objections & Testimonials
*(→ Customer Saga Section 8: Objections)*

39. What are the most common reasons people hesitate before working with you?
    *(Worried about being pushed? Not sure if they need an agent? Seen bad agents before?)*
40. What do you say when someone says "I'll just search on MagicBricks / 99acres myself"?
41. What do you say when someone asks "How do I know you're showing me what's best for me, not what gives you the highest commission?"
42. What do you say to someone worried about legal issues or title disputes?
43. What do you say to someone who's been cheated by a builder or agent before?
44. Do you have testimonials, client reviews, or successful case stories you can share?

---

## ⚠️ PART 9 — Stakes & Consequences
*(→ Customer Saga Section 9: Stakes)*

45. What goes wrong when someone buys or sells property without a trustworthy agent?
    *(Overpaid? Legal disputes later? Wrong locality? Encumbered title?)*
46. What is the real long-term cost of a bad property decision beyond money?
    *(Years of EMI on a property that doesn't appreciate? Stuck in a legal battle?)*
47. What is the best-case scenario when someone works with you and everything goes right?
