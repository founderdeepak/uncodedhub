# 🎯 Coach & Consultant — Client Discovery Questionnaire
### Uncoded Hub | Website + Customer Saga + Google Business + Brochure
*75 Questions | Map every answer directly to the Customer Saga Workbook Sections 1–10*

---

## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is your full name and business / brand name?

2. Where are you based? Do you work with clients locally, across India, or internationally?

3. How long have you been coaching or consulting professionally?

4. What is your background before coaching / consulting?
   *(Corporate career? Entrepreneur? Domain expert? Academic?)*

5. What type of coaching or consulting do you offer?
   - [ ] Business coaching (for entrepreneurs / founders)
   - [ ] Life coaching / mindset coaching
   - [ ] Career coaching (job seekers, career changers)
   - [ ] Executive / leadership coaching (corporate professionals)
   - [ ] Sales coaching or training
   - [ ] Marketing / personal branding consulting
   - [ ] Financial coaching / wealth management consulting
   - [ ] Health, wellness, or fitness coaching
   - [ ] Relationship / marriage coaching
   - [ ] Niche domain consulting (specify: ___________)
   - [ ] Other: ___________

6. Who is your ideal client?
   - [ ] Founders / startup entrepreneurs
   - [ ] Small business owners
   - [ ] Corporate mid-managers wanting to grow
   - [ ] Senior executives / C-suite leaders
   - [ ] Job seekers / career transitioners
   - [ ] Students and fresh graduates
   - [ ] Women entrepreneurs / professionals
   - [ ] NRI clients
   - [ ] Other: ___________

7. What is your average client investment? (e.g., ₹15K for a workshop, ₹50K for a 3-month programme, ₹2L+ for a high-ticket retainer)

8. How many clients have you coached or consulted to date? (approximate)

9. Do you work solo or do you have a team? (Support staff? Co-coaches? Assistants?)

## 💛 PART 2 — What Clients Want
*(→ Customer Saga Section 2: Desires)*

10. What does your ideal client ultimately want beyond a coaching or consulting session?
    *(A promotion? A thriving business? Inner peace? A career they love? Financial freedom?)*

11. What emotion do your clients want to feel 3 months after working with you?

12. What does the "dream life or business" look like for the person you most love helping?

---

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

13. What are the biggest complaints clients have about other coaches or consultants they've tried before?
    *(Too generic? Just motivational fluff? No accountability? Cookie-cutter advice? Disappeared after payment?)*

14. What mistakes do bad coaches or consultants make that you would never make?

15. Have you ever had a client come to you after a bad experience with another coach? What was the story?

16. What is the most painful or frustrating moment in your client's journey — the thing that finally makes them reach out?

17. What do clients NOT know about the coaching / consulting process before they start — things that shape expectations?

---

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What is your coaching or consulting philosophy — how do you approach the work differently?
    *(Results-first? Mindset-first? Systems-based? Experience-based? Spiritual approach?)*

19. What methodology, framework, or proprietary process do you use?
    *(Do you have a named framework, a signature system, or a unique way of working?)*

20. What credentials, certifications, or training do you hold?
    *(ICF certified? PCC / MCC? MBA? IIT/IIM background? Industry-specific credentials?)*

21. What real-world experience do you bring that most coaches or consultants in your niche don't?
    *(Built and exited a company? Led a team of 500? Turned around a failing business?)*

22. Do you offer any guarantee — outcome, satisfaction, or refund policy?

23. What is your most impressive client transformation? Tell the full story.
    *(Who was the client? Where were they when they started? Where are they now? What changed?)*

24. List your TOP 5 client success stories / case studies:
    - Client profile (industry / role — anonymised if needed)
    - Starting situation
    - What you did
    - Result / outcome
    - Can you share this publicly? (YES / NO)

25. Do you have any speaking engagements, published books, articles, podcasts, or media appearances?
    *(Links or names)*

26. Have you been featured in press, business media, or popular publications?
    *(e.g., YourStory, Economic Times, Forbes India, podcasts — links)*

27. What do your clients say about you most often after the engagement ends? (Actual quotes)

28. Do you have written testimonials, LinkedIn recommendations, Google reviews, or video testimonials?

    2. Discovery / strategy call →
    3. Proposal / programme match →
    4. Onboarding and payment →
    5. Sessions begin →
    6. Ongoing accountability and support →
    7. Programme completion and next steps →

33. What formats do you deliver your service in?
    - [ ] 1:1 coaching calls (video / phone)
    - [ ] Group coaching programme
    - [ ] Online course or curriculum
    - [ ] In-person intensive / workshop
    - [ ] Hybrid (mix of above)
    - [ ] Retainer / ongoing advisory

34. How often do clients work with you? (Weekly calls? Bi-weekly? Monthly check-ins?)

35. What is your pricing structure?
    *(Per session? Monthly retainer? Programme package? One-time intensive?)*

## 🙅 PART 8 — Objections & Testimonials
*(→ Customer Saga Section 8: Objections)*

41. What do you say when someone says "I've tried programmes before and they didn't work."
