# 🏠 Interior Designer & Architect — Client Discovery Questionnaire
### Uncoded Hub | Website + Customer Saga + Google Business + Brochure
*75 Questions | Map every answer directly to the Customer Saga Workbook Sections 1–10*

---

## 🏛️ PART 1 — About the Business
*(→ Customer Saga Section 1: Perfect Client Avatar)*

1. What is your full name and business/studio name?
2. Where are you based? Which cities do you take projects in?
3. How long have you been practising interior design or architecture?
4. How did you get started in this field? What is your background or training?
5. What type of work do you primarily do?
   - [ ] Residential interiors (apartments, villas, bungalows)
   - [ ] Commercial interiors (offices, retail, hospitality)
   - [ ] Architectural design and planning
   - [ ] Turnkey interior projects (design + execution both)
   - [ ] Renovation and remodelling only
   - [ ] Modular furniture and kitchen design
   - [ ] Landscape and exterior design
   - [ ] Other: ___________
6. Who is your typical client?
   - [ ] Homeowners (new flat/villa purchase)
   - [ ] Homeowners doing renovation
   - [ ] Young working couples
   - [ ] HNI / luxury segment clients
   - [ ] Builders / developers (model flat work)
   - [ ] Business owners (office, showroom, restaurant)
   - [ ] Other: ___________
7. What is the average project budget range you work with? (e.g., ₹5L–₹15L for 2BHK, ₹30L+ for luxury villas)
8. How many projects have you completed to date? (approximate)
9. Do you work solo or do you have a team? (Designer + site supervisor + vendor network?)

## 💛 PART 2 — What Clients Want
*(→ Customer Saga Section 2: Desires)*

10. What is the #1 thing your clients ultimately want beyond just a beautiful interior?
    *(A home that feels like them? A space that impresses guests? A stress-free process?)*
11. What emotion do clients want to feel when they walk into their finished space?
12. What does the "dream outcome" look like for your ideal client?

---

## 😤 PART 3 — Client's Problem
*(→ Customer Saga Section 3: Big Problem + Symptoms)*

13. What are the most common complaints clients have about OTHER interior designers they have worked with before?
14. What mistakes do bad designers make that you never make?
15. Have you ever rescued a project gone wrong by another designer? What happened?
16. What is the most stressful part of the process for a client doing interior work?
17. What do clients typically NOT know about interior projects before they start — things that shock them later?

---

## 🏆 PART 4 — Your Solution & Authority
*(→ Customer Saga Section 4: Solution + Brag Board)*

18. What is your design philosophy? What makes your work distinctly yours?
19. What is your style signature — what visual identity do you always bring to your work?
    *(Minimalist? Maximalist? Contemporary? Traditional? Fusion? Biophilic?)*
20. Do you offer 3D visualisations / renders before work begins? What software do you use?
21. Do you handle procurement and vendor management, or does the client manage that?
22. Do you offer a turnkey model — single point of contact for everything?
23. What is your most impressive completed project? Tell the full story.
    *(What was it, who was the client, what was special about it?)*
24. List your TOP 5 completed projects:
    - Project name / type
    - Location (city / area)
    - Year completed
    - Budget range
    - Photos available? (YES / NO)
25. Do you have any certifications, design school credentials, or industry memberships?
    *(IID, CII, CIDA, B.Arch, M.Des?)*
26. Have any of your projects been featured in magazines, blogs, or social media?
    *(Links or names)*
27. What do clients say most often after the project is complete? (Actual quotes)
28. Do you have written testimonials, Google reviews, or Instagram DMs from happy clients?

## 📣 PART 5 — Call to Action
*(→ Customer Saga Section 5: CTA)*

29. How should new clients first reach you? (Call / WhatsApp / Fill a form / Book a consultation)
30. What is your consultation process — is the first meeting free or paid?
31. What information does a client need to share before you can give them a rough estimate?

## ⚙️ PART 6 — Your Process
*(→ Customer Saga Section 6: 3-Step Process)*

32. Walk me through your full project process — step by step:
    1. First enquiry →
    2. Consultation / site visit →
    3. Design concept presentation →
    4. Design approval and agreement →
    5. Execution / site work begins →
    6. Progress reviews →
    7. Final handover →
33. How long does a typical residential project take from start to handover?
34. How do you keep clients updated during the execution phase?
35. What is your payment structure? (Design fee structure + execution payment milestones)

## 🙅 PART 8 — Objections & Testimonials
*(→ Customer Saga Section 8: Objections)*

    *(Too expensive? Unsure if they need a designer? Worried about timeline going over?)*
40. What do you say when someone asks "Can't I just do this myself or hire a contractor directly?"
41. What do you say when someone says "Your fees are higher than other designers I've spoken to"?
42. What do you say to someone worried that the project will go over budget?
43. What do you say to someone who has been burned by a designer before?
44. Do you have testimonials, client reviews, or before-after photos you can share?

## ⚠️ PART 9 — Stakes & Consequences
*(→ Customer Saga Section 9: Stakes)*

45. What goes wrong when someone tries to do interiors without a professional designer?
    *(Wrong measurements? Incompatible materials? Cost overruns? Regret later?)*

    *(Living in a space that doesn't work, daily frustration, resale value impact?)*
47. What is the best-case scenario when someone works with you? What does their life look like 6 months after handover?
