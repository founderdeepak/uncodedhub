Frequently Answered Questions

What CAD and drawing formats do you accept for RFQ review? +
We accept STEP (.stp, .step), IGES, Parasolid (.x_t), and native SolidWorks files along with 2D engineering PDF drawings indicating critical GD&T dimensions and surface finish requirements.
What quality documentation accompanies production shipments? +
Every production batch includes a Certificate of Conformance (CoC), Raw Material Mill Test Reports (chemical & mechanical properties), Zeiss CMM full-dimensional inspection report (FAI according to AS9102), and heat treatment/surface treatment compliance certificates.
Can you sign Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDA) prior to receiving drawings? +
Yes. We routinely execute bilateral NDAs with defense, space, and medical OEMs within 4 hours. All CAD files are stored on isolated, encrypted servers behind ITAR-compliant firewalls.
What is your typical turnaround for rapid prototypes? +
For standard alloys (Al 6061/7075, SS304/316) in inventory, rapid prototype first-articles can be programmed, 5-axis milled, CMM inspected, and dispatched within 3 to 5 working days.
