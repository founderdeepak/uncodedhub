Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

How do you safeguard fragile glassware and crockery?
We pack each individual item in honeycomb wrapping paper, layer them inside cellular divider boxes, and place them into double-walled corrugated outer cartons.
Do your movers dismantle and reassemble large furniture?
Yes. Our moving crew includes experienced carpenters equipped with power tools to dismantle hydraulic beds, wardrobes, and dining tables, and reassemble them at your new destination.
Is transit insurance mandatory and what does it cover?
Transit insurance covers accidents, fire, overturning, and catastrophic transit losses. We arrange immediate all-risk policy generation prior to dispatch.
