Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

How is a 0.01D wavefront exam different from ordinary 0.25D testing?
Traditional opticians test in coarse 0.25 steps (e.g. -1.00, -1.25). Our Zeiss wavefront technology measures your eyes in micro-steps of 0.01 diopters, matching your eye’s biological fingerprint for razor-sharp clarity.
What does your 1-year lens warranty cover?
If your anti-reflective coating peels or if deep accidental scratches develop during normal wear within 365 days, we replace both lenses free of charge with zero hassles.
Can I bring my own existing frame for new lens fitting?
Yes. Our in-house optical edging laboratory can custom-cut and surface Zeiss or Essilor lenses to fit your existing beloved frames with 24-hour turnaround.
