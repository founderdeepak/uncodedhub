The Photographer & Printing Engineer

Dhananjay Joshi

"A photograph is only half-born when the shutter clicks. It achieves immortality when ink meets paper with engineered precision."

With a professional background as a printing engineer holding over 25 years of mastery in commercial printing and packaging, Dhananjay Joshi brings a rare scientific discipline to wildlife fine art.

Since turning full-time to wildlife exploration, he has documented over 1.5 million frames across the wilderness of India and Africa. His works have garnered prestigious national and international photography awards, and he regularly mentors wildlife enthusiasts through guided photographic expeditions.
