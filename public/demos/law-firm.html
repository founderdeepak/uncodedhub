Bar Council of India Ethics & Practice FAQ

Clear answers regarding regulatory compliance, conflict-of-interest verifications, and professional standards governing Vanguard Chambers.

How does the Bar Council of India Rule 36 govern this website? +
In strict compliance with Rule 36 of the Bar Council of India Rules, this digital portal operates exclusively as an informational knowledge repository and confidential conflict-check clearance registry for clients seeking legal assistance. Vanguard Chambers does not solicit briefs or advertise its services. Information is furnished solely upon the specific request of the prospective client.
What is the adverse party conflict clearance procedure? +
Before accepting any brief, receiving confidential case records, or quoting a retainer, Vanguard Chambers runs an automated and manual registry search against all past, present, and advised adverse corporate entities. A formal negative conflict clearance certificate is issued within 24 hours of submission.
How are milestone retainers structured compared to hourly billing? +
We reject the opaque 6-minute increment model. Matters are contracted on transparent milestone phases: (1) Research, Pleadings & Injunction Filing, (2) Admission & Interim Argument, and (3) Final Evidentiary Hearing and Disposal. This provides corporate boards and general counsels with 100% budget predictability.
How quickly can Vanguard Chambers move for ex-parte ad-interim injunctions? +
For urgent commercial matters involving asset dissipation, unauthorized invocation of bank guarantees, or trade secret breaches, our chambers maintain a 24/7 emergency listing protocol. We are capable of settling petitions overnight and mentioning before vacation benches by 10:30 AM the following morning.
