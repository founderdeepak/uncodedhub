Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

Do we need to empty our kitchen cabinets before treatment?
No. Our advanced gel formulations are odorless and applied in tiny, targeted droplets in hidden corners and drawer tracks. You do not need to empty dishes or leave the house.
Are your chemicals safe for infant children and household pets?
Yes. We exclusively deploy WHO Class III and CIB-registered chemical formulations from Bayer and Syngenta that are specifically approved for use in hospitals and homes with pets.
What happens if pests reappear after treatment?
Our Annual AMC and warranty services include a 100% free re-treatment policy. Call us or message on WhatsApp, and a technician will visit at zero cost.
