Frequently Answered Questions

How early should we engage your team for a destination wedding? +
For prime heritage palace venues in Rajasthan or peak season dates in Goa (November to February), we recommend engaging our planning team 6 to 9 months in advance to lock preferred hotel room blocks and venue buyout contracts at optimal rates.
Do you handle venue negotiation and hotel room contracts? +
Yes. We leverage our institutional relationships with luxury hotel chains (Taj, Oberoi, Leela, Marriott) to negotiate room block rates, attrition allowances, corkage waivers, and favorable food & beverage buyout clauses on your family's behalf.
What happens if unexpected weather occurs at an outdoor venue? +
Every outdoor design plan includes an architectural "Plan B" rain-proof contingency. We reserve modular German waterproof clear-span hanger structures and have our structural rigging crew on standby 24 hours prior to the event.
Can you liaise with celebrity singers, DJs, and choreographers? +
Yes. We directly manage artist technical riders, hospitality suites, sound engineering rehearsals, and travel itineraries for top Bollywood playback artists, global DJs, and master sangeet choreographers.
