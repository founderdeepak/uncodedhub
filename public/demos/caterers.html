Frequently Answered Questions

Can we schedule a private tasting before confirming our wedding booking? +
Yes. We host dedicated private family tasting sessions in our Indiranagar executive tasting studio for up to 6 family members. You taste every starter, live counter item, main course, and dessert before making any contract commitment.
How do you guarantee that food will never run out if extra guests arrive? +
We maintain an automatic 10% safety buffer on raw materials and semi-prepared bases stored in on-site refrigerated support vans. If your guest count swells beyond RSVP, our production team seamlessly scales without any noticeable disruption.
Do you provide trained service staff and luxury tableware? +
Yes. Every catering tier includes fully uniformed service staff, professional banquet captains, bone china or brass tableware, fine glassware, linen napkins, and dedicated cleanup teams.
Can you accommodate strictly separate vegetarian and Jain kitchens? +
Yes. We maintain completely segregated kitchen cooking equipment, refrigeration lines, prep tables, and dedicated chef teams for pure vegetarian and Jain menus to guarantee absolute culinary sanctity.
