Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

How do you prevent water seepage on terrace gardens?
We apply a triple-layer protection system: elastomeric waterproofing, 30mm heavy-duty dimpled drain cells, and geotextile filtration fabric so soil never touches the concrete slab.
Will the plants survive the hot summer seasons?
Yes. We select native, climate-hardy species suited for Indian microclimates and pair them with smart automated drip irrigation programmed for early morning watering cycles.
Do you offer monthly horticultural maintenance after handover?
Yes. Our GreenThumb AMC includes twice-a-week gardener visits for pruning, organic fertilization, neem oil pest prevention, and seasonal flower replanting.
