Frequently Answered Questions

How do you guarantee zero budget overruns on turnkey contracts? +
We operate on a legally binding Lump-Sum Turnkey (LSTK) contract with fully itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ). Before ground-breaking, our structural engineering team validates soil bearing capacity, MEP load calculations, and structural detailing down to the kilogram of steel. Any internal material price fluctuation is absorbed by us, protecting your investment.
What happens if the project completion is delayed? +
Our contracts include a standard Liquidated Damages (LD) clause: 0.5% of contract value per week of unexcused delay, capped at 10%. We have delivered 100% of our last 14 institutional and residential projects within the promised timeline.
Do you handle municipal sanctions and RERA clearances? +
Yes. Our in-house liaison engineering division manages BBMP/BDA plan sanctions, BESCOM power transformer approvals, BWSSB sanitary clearances, and Fire NOC procurement as part of the turnkey engagement.
How do owners track progress remotely? +
Every client receives access to our Apex Project Portal with daily 4K drone photography, time-lapse logs, material testing lab reports (cube test compressive strength certificates), and milestone billing verification.
