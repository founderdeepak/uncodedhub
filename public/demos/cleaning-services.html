Frequently Answered Questions

Can cleaning be scheduled outside office hours to avoid disrupting staff? +
Yes. Over 85% of our corporate contracts are serviced during night shifts (8:00 PM to 5:00 AM) or over weekends. Our teams are equipped with quiet HEPA vacuums and supervised by biometric-tracked site managers.
Are your cleaning staff directly employed and background verified? +
Yes. 100% of our facility personnel are direct payroll employees with police background verification, Aadhaar biometric authentication, and active PF/ESI compliance.
Are the chemicals safe for employees with allergies or asthma? +
We exclusively use Green Seal and EPA-registered eco-friendly botanical or hydrogen peroxide formulations that break down into water and oxygen within minutes, leaving zero toxic fumes, chlorine odors, or VOC irritants.
How do you objectively verify that surfaces are truly sterilized? +
We conduct before-and-after ATP (Adenosine Triphosphate) bioluminescence swab testing on random high-touch surfaces (door handles, elevator buttons, keyboards). Results are documented in a digitally signed hygiene certificate.
