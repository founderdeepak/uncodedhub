Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

Can we monitor our security cameras when away from the office?
Yes. Every installation includes encrypted P2P mobile app and PC client configuration, allowing live multi-screen viewing, playback, and incident clipping from anywhere in the world.
What happens to recordings during power cuts?
We configure dedicated online UPS battery systems for the NVR, PoE switches, and cameras, ensuring uninterrupted 4-to-8 hour recording during grid failures.
Do you offer an annual maintenance contract (AMC)?
Yes. Our Security AMC includes quarterly camera lens cleaning, cabling integrity testing, hard drive health audits, firmware security patches, and 4-hour breakdown support.
