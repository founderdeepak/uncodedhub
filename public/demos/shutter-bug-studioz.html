FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to help you plan your visit.

Quick clarifications on timing, photo submissions, and studio appointments.

Do I need an appointment for Visa or Passport photos?

No appointment is necessary! You can simply walk into our Malleshpalya studio during working hours (9:30 AM – 9:30 PM). Your photos will be shot, cropped to country specs, printed, and delivered in under 10 minutes.

How long does a baby or toddler photoshoot take?

We typically allocate 1 to 2 hours with zero rush. We understand children need time to get comfortable, feed, or change outfits. Our studio is temperature-controlled and sanitized specifically for newborns and toddlers.

Can I bring existing digital photos for custom framing?

Yes, absolutely. You can bring your photos on a pen drive or simply WhatsApp them to us. Our lab will assess resolution, enhance colors if needed, and assist you in selecting the ideal moulding and matboard size.

What if my visa photo gets rejected by the embassy?

We offer a strict 100% Embassy Acceptance Guarantee. If any consulate or visa center flags your photo, we will re-shoot and provide new physical and digital sets immediately at zero additional cost.

How quickly can I get custom frames or 3D crystals?

Standard framing and canvas prints are completed in-house within 2 to 3 days. Express same-day or 24-hour turnaround is also available for urgent gifting requirements upon request.

What are your studio timings and parking options?

We are open Monday through Saturday from 9:30 AM to 9:30 PM, and Sundays from 10:30 AM to 9:30 PM. Convenient two-wheeler and four-wheeler parking is available right outside the studio beside Apple Clinic on Malleshpalya Main Road.
