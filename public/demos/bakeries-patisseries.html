Bakery Guidelines

Frequently Answered Questions

How far in advance do I need to order a custom celebration cake? +
For 1-tier and 2-tier celebration cakes, we require a minimum of 48 hours notice. For 3-tier wedding cakes and custom dessert tables, we recommend booking 2 to 4 weeks in advance to secure date availability and schedule a private tasting box.
Do you offer 100% eggless options for breads and celebration cakes? +
Yes. All our wild sourdough hearth loaves (country, rye, seeded, focaccia) are naturally 100% vegan and eggless. For celebration cakes, we offer dedicated eggless sponge formulations that achieve identical moisture and tender crumb using organic curd and fruit purée reductions.
How does cake delivery work to ensure tiers don't collapse? +
All tiered celebration cakes are reinforced with internal food-safe structural dowels and transported inside specialized climate-controlled refrigerated vehicles. Our senior pastry chef personally conducts venue setup and floral placement on-site.
How should I store authentic sourdough at home? +
Never store sourdough bread in the refrigerator, as cold temperatures accelerate retrogradation (staling). Keep the loaf cut-side down on a wooden breadboard or inside a linen bread bag. On days 3 to 5, toast slices with salted butter for peak flavor.
