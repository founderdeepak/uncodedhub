Frequently Answered Questions

Can I book a table in advance for weekend evenings? +
Yes. We accept table reservations for parties of 4 or more up to 7:30 PM on weekends through our direct WhatsApp link. For walk-ins, our beer garden hosts a comfortable bar lounge while your outdoor table is prepared.
Is your beer garden pet-friendly? +
100% pet-friendly. Our open-air courtyard welcomes leashed dogs, and we provide complimentary water bowls and unseasoned smoked bone treats prepared fresh daily by our pitmaster.
Do you offer growlers or canned craft beer to take home? +
Subject to prevailing excise regulations, we offer counter-pressure filled 1-litre amber glass growlers purged with CO2 to ensure your beer stays fresh and bubbly for up to 72 hours in your home refrigerator.
Can we host corporate gatherings or buy out the roof deck? +
Yes. Our mezzanine and rooftop terrace can accommodate private groups of 30 to 120 guests with dedicated craft taps, personalized menus, and audio-visual projector setups for company milestones.
