// CULINARY MANIFESTO
"Gas stoves are for boiling water. Real flavor comes from the soul of timber, the alchemy of hot embers, and the patience of slow smoke."

Trained under master charcoal cooks across Kashmir, Lucknow, and the Konkan coast, Chef Kabir Saxena built Saffron & Smoke on a radical refusal to take shortcuts. In an era where commercial kitchens microwave pre-cooked gravies and use artificial liquid smoke, every single skewer and curry pot here is cooked over living seasoned embers.

01 // Zero Gas Burners
Our main cooking line runs 100% on seasoned oak, babool wood, and coconut-shell charcoal.
02 // 48-Hour Marinades
Meats and coastal seafood rest in stone-ground spices, cold-pressed mustard oil, and hung curd for 48 hours.
03 // Fresh Catch Boat Sourcing
We source directly from artisanal fishermen in Mangalore and Malvan, flown in fresh daily on ice.
04 // Zero Middleman Fees
We pass the 30% commission saved from delivery platforms into better farm ingredients and guest savings.
