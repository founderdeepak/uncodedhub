What if I am very nervous about driving in Bangalore traffic?
Our cars are fitted with dual pedals (clutch and brake on the passenger side). The trainer has full override authority so there is zero risk of collision. We start in empty private grounds before touching city roads.
Do you offer doorstep pickup and drop-off?
Yes! For all 15-day and 25-day programs, your instructor picks you up directly from your apartment gate or office lobby.
Will you accompany me on the official RTO test day?
Yes. Our senior instructor accompanies you in the exact same dual-control car you practiced with, ensuring total familiarity on the automated track.
