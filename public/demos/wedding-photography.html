Your wedding is the single most emotionally significant weekend of your life. Don't let an assembly-line photography agency treat it like an invoice.

1

The Associate Bait-and-Switch

Large agencies show you breathtaking photos shot by their celebrity founder, but on your wedding morning, two college interns show up with gear they rented that week.

The 6-Month Delivery Void
