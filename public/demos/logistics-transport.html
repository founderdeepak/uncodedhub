Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

Are your vehicles IBA approved for bank bill discount?
Yes, FleetPro Logistics is an IBA-approved transporter. Our consignor lorry receipts (LR) are recognized by all scheduled public and private commercial banks.
Do you provide live GPS tracking access for consignees?
Yes. Upon vehicle dispatch, you and your receiving dock manager receive an automated web tracking link with real-time location, speed, and ETA updates.
What is your procedure in case of highway breakdown?
We operate 24/7 highway response teams with 35+ partner garages along Golden Quadrilateral highways. Replacement tractor units are placed within 3 hours.
