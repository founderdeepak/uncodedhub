Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

Can parents really watch their children on live CCTV?
Yes. Every enrolled parent receives secure, encrypted mobile app credentials to watch their child’s classroom, nap room, and play yard during operational hours.
What is your sick-child and illness containment policy?
We conduct temperature checks at the front gate every morning. Children with fever or contagious symptoms are rested in our comfortable isolation infirmary until parents arrive.
Are meals cooked fresh on-premises?
Yes. 100% of meals are prepared fresh in our open, stainless-steel commercial kitchen using RO-filtered water, organic whole grains, and seasonal vegetables with zero refined sugar.
