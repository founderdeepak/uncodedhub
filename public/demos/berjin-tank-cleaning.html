Our 6-stage deep cleaning methodology.

We never use toxic harsh acids or amateur mop cleaning. Our mechanized process guarantees 100% pure water.

01

Mechanized Dewatering

High-capacity automatic submersible pumps drain stagnant dirty water from the tank down to the last millimeter without flooding your terrace.

02

Sludge & Silt Extraction

Specialized heavy-duty industrial vacuum cleaners extract thick layers of settled mud, iron rust, and dirt from deep tank corners.

03

High-Pressure Rotary Jet Wash

Rotary high-pressure water nozzles strip away stubborn algae, calcified moss, and bio-film from walls and ceilings without scrubbing damage.

04

Vacuum Slurry Removal

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions about tank & pool cleaning.

Everything you need to know before booking your cleaning appointment.

How long does water tank cleaning take?

For standard overhead plastic tanks (500L–2,000L), our mechanized 6-stage cleaning takes approximately 45 to 60 minutes. Underground concrete sumps typically take 1.5 to 3 hours depending on capacity.

Do I need to empty the tank before you arrive?

No, please do not empty it completely! We use high-capacity automatic submersible pumps to drain the residual dirty water, and we utilize a small bucket of water for our pressure jet machinery.

Are the cleaning chemicals safe for drinking water?

Yes, absolutely. We strictly use food-grade, government-approved antibacterial sanitizers that leave zero toxic chemical odor or taste. The tank can be refilled with drinking water immediately after cleaning.

How often should a water tank be cleaned?

Health and municipal standards recommend cleaning overhead tanks and underground sumps at least once every 6 months to prevent toxic bacterial biofilm, sludge, and mosquito breeding.

What swimming pool cleaning services do you provide?

We provide deep scrubbing, underwater vacuuming, tile stain descaling, backwash filtration, and water chemical rebalancing (pH, chlorine, clarifier). Both one-time cleans and monthly AMC contracts are available.

Do you offer discounts for apartments & bulk tanks?

Yes! We offer attractive group discount pricing for apartment complexes, gated villa communities, and commercial facilities when multiple tanks or sumps are booked together.
