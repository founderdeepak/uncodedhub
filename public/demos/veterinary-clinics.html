Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers to the most common client questions.

Are your doctors on-site during late night hours?
Yes. Unlike day clinics with doctors merely on-call, Paws & Claws has licensed veterinary surgeons, veterinary nurses, and lab technicians physically present in the hospital 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
Do you have separate spaces for cats and dogs?
Yes! We follow Fear-Free certified guidelines with completely separate waiting areas, examination rooms, and inpatient recovery wards so felines never experience canine barking or scent stress.
Can I stay with my pet during minor procedures and checkups?
Absolutely. We encourage pet parents to accompany their companions during consultations, ultrasound scans, and vaccinations to keep pets calm and comforted.
