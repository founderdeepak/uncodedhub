Frequently Answered Questions

What is the difference between a SEBI Registered Investment Adviser and a Mutual Fund Distributor? +
Mutual Fund Distributors (MFDs) and private banks sell products on commission. They receive an ongoing 1% to 2% trail fee every single year out of your wealth, regardless of whether your portfolio gains or loses. As a SEBI Registered RIA, we are legally bound by a fiduciary duty: we are prohibited from accepting any commissions or kickbacks from any AMC or product provider. We are paid solely by you.
Do you ever take custody of my investment money? +
Never. Your funds and securities always remain in your personal Demat accounts and bank accounts at institutional custodians (such as Zerodha, Groww, ICICI Direct, or NSDL/CDSL). We only provide advisory guidance and asset allocation instructions which you approve.
What is your minimum investable asset threshold? +
Our comprehensive multi-family office and private wealth mandate begins at an investable corpus of ₹1 Crore. For early-stage founders and executives with upcoming liquidity events, we offer modular financial planning sprints.
How do you handle capital gains and taxes when switching from regular plans? +
We conduct a granular tax-cost analysis before making any switches. We stagger transitions over multiple financial years to utilize annual ₹1.25 Lakh long-term capital gain exemptions and offset against existing carried-forward capital losses.
