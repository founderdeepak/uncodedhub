## 8. The 3 Biggest Objections & Bulletproof Rebuttals

### 🔴 Objection: *"All my brides come through Instagram; I don't need a website"*
**🟢 Uncoded Hub Rebuttal:**
> *"Brides find you on Instagram, but when they want to show your work to their mother or groom to approve a ₹60,000 booking, an Instagram link gets lost in the feed. An official atelier site anchors your brand, allows them to book a paid trial, and eliminates price haggling."*

> *"You don't publish a flat rate; you showcase your tiered experiences (e.g., HD Signature vs Airbrush Couture) with inclusions (hair draping, lashes, trial). It pre-qualifies their budget so you stop wasting hours on brides with ₹5,000 expectations."*
