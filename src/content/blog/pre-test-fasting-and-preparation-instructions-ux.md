## Frequently asked questions

**Why are clear digital pre-test preparation instructions crucial for diagnostic accuracy?**  
Inaccurate patient fasting (such as consuming milk coffee before lipid profiles or taking thyroid medication prior to TSH draws) ruins sample integrity, resulting in invalid results and costly re-sampling visits.

**How should pre-test instructions be formatted on test detail pages?**  
Use prominent visual checklists: hours of overnight fasting required, water consumption allowances, medication withholding guidelines, and urine/stool collection sterile container protocols.

**How can labs automate pre-test reminders to reduce invalid samples?**  
Integrate automated SMS and WhatsApp reminders dispatched 14 hours and 2 hours prior to scheduled collection, reinforcing fasting instructions directly to the patient's lock screen.
