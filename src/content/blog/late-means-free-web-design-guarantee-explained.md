## 1. Why Traditional Agencies Cannot Offer Guarantees

To understand why traditional agencies treat delivery dates as vague suggestions, look at their organizational structure:

```
[The Bloated Traditional Agency Pipeline]
Sales Executive Pitch ➔ Account Manager Briefing ➔ Junior UI Designer
➔ Art Director Review ➔ Outsourced Freelance Developer ➔ QA Intern
➔ Account Manager Client Review ➔ Client Feedback ➔ Re-Brief Cycle
```

Every handoff in this bloated chain introduces:
- Context loss and miscommunication
- Days of idle waiting in internal review queues
- Zero direct accountability (the designer blames the developer; the developer blames the client brief)

By the time the project reaches Day 30, nobody in the agency actually owns the final outcome.
