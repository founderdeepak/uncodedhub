The higher you climb in corporate advisory and C-suite coaching, the less your clients want their names publicly broadcast on the internet.

A Fortune 500 CEO working with an executive coach to navigate board conflicts, panic attacks, or succession planning will never permit their photo and name to appear in a public testimonial block. A private equity firm hiring a consultant to manage a hostile acquisition enforces non-negotiable Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

This presents an apparent paradox for the consultant: **How do you demonstrate credibility to prospective enterprise buyers when your most impressive work is legally confidential?**

Junior consultants make the error of complaining that they have no social proof. Master advisors deploy **Blinded Case Study Frameworks** that protect client privacy while providing overwhelming technical evidence of competence.

---

## 1. The Anatomy of an Anonymized "Blinded" Case Study

A blinded case study replaces identifying trade secrets with precise structural characteristics:

```
[Blinded Case Study Architecture]
├── Industry Identifier: Tier-1 Indian EdTech Unicorn ($1.2B Valuation)
├── Organizational Scope: 850 Engineers, 4 Product Verticals
├── The Confidential Mandate: Executive conflict between Co-founders and Lead Investors
├── Diagnostic Findings: Alignment failure across equity vesting and product roadmap
├── Tactical Strategy: 12-week mediation framework and Board Governance Matrix
└── Concrete Business Result: Resolved impasse, secured $40M Series C, zero executive departures
```

Notice the level of operational specificity. By articulating the exact dynamics of the conflict, the governance hurdles, and the measurable business outcome, prospective executives recognize their own challenges in your words.

---

## 2. The 3 Safe Levels of Proof Attribution

When gathering executive proof, offer your clients three tiers of attribution:

| Proof Tier | When to Deploy | Format Displayed on Website |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Fully Attributed** | Public corporate transformations | Full Name, Official Title, Company Logo, Video Interview |
| **Tier 2: Role-Attributed** | Sensitive internal coaching | "VP of Global Sales, NASDAQ-listed Cloud Platform" |
| **Tier 3: Blinded Composite** | Turnarounds, M&A, Crisis Advisory | "Managing Director, Top-3 Indian Private Equity Fund" |

Most enterprise executives who decline a Tier 1 public testimonial will gladly agree to a Tier 2 role-attributed endorsement because it honors their contribution without exposing their company to public scrutiny.

---

## 3. Highlighting Your Strict Code of Confidentiality

Turn your respect for NDAs into an institutional asset. Feature a prominent **Client Confidentiality Charter** on your website:
- *"We operate under strict legal NDAs. We never share client rosters, proprietary internal metrics, or board proceedings. When you work with our advisory firm, your operational vulnerabilities remain 100% confidential."*

Enterprise decision-makers view this commitment with immense relief. A consultant who carelessly name-drops past clients on their website is seen as a liability; a consultant who fiercely guards client privacy is seen as a trusted confidant.
