## Frequently asked questions

**Why are unretouched before-and-after sliders essential for luxury bridal beauty websites?**  
Modern brides are hyper-aware of deceptive Instagram filters. Demonstrating real, unretouched skin texture under natural daylight builds overwhelming trust that makeup will look flawless in person, not just on a phone screen.

**How do interactive sliders outperform static side-by-side photos?**  
Interactive sliders let brides control the reveal with their finger, examining the seamless blending of foundation, precision eyeliner, and natural contouring in high-definition detail.

**What lighting conditions should be used when photographing bridal transformations?**  
Photograph under true 5500K daylight and warm indoor event lighting to show how the makeup holds up across daytime ceremonies and evening receptions.
