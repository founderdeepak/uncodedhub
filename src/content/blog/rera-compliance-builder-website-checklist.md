# RERA Compliance on Builder Websites: What Small Developers Must Display

*By [Deepak](https://in.linkedin.com/in/deepakdeveloper), co-founder of Uncoded Hub, where he leads sales and strategy. Verified as of September 26, 2026.*

Every property buyer in India has heard a horror story about delayed handovers, unapproved floor additions, and disputed land titles.

Because of this history, the first thing an intelligent homebuyer looks for on a builder's website is not the swimming pool render. It is the RERA registration number.

If that number is missing, buried in six-point grey type in the footer, or links to an error page, the buyer does not call your sales team to ask for clarification. They assume the project has title disputes or pending municipal sanctions, and they close the tab.

For a boutique builder or independent developer, legal transparency is not a compliance chore. It is your single most effective sales weapon against fly-by-night competitors.

## The short answer

Section 11(2) of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act mandates that all project advertisements—including websites and landing pages—must prominently display the RERA registration number and the official state RERA portal URL. For small developers, placing verified RERA credentials, sanctioned architectural layouts, quarterly construction progress photos, and true RERA carpet areas directly in the header and project hero disarms buyer anxiety and accelerates booking decisions.

## Why hiding legal disclosures destroys buyer trust

In [*Never Split the Difference*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/26156469-never-split-the-difference), Chris Voss writes that empathy means understanding your counterparty's deepest anxieties before attempting to persuade them.

A homebuyer committing eighty lakhs or two crores of their life savings experiences acute risk aversion. They are terrified of buying into a project that stalls at the foundation stage.

When a builder's website uses vague marketing terms like *"super built-up area"* while hiding the actual RERA carpet area, the buyer senses manipulation. As Robert Cialdini demonstrates in [*Pre-Suasion*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/29363999-pre-suasion), trustworthiness is established before the core message is delivered. Stating your legal approvals up front frames your entire project as legitimate and safe.

## The 5 essential RERA elements every builder site must feature

### 1. Persistent RERA Registration in the Header and Hero
Do not relegate your RERA number to the footer copyright line. Display it prominently in the hero section directly below the project name:
- **Project Name:** *Greenwood Terraces, Sarjapur*
- **PRM Registration:** *PRM/KA/RERA/1251/308/PR/220126/004512*
- **Direct Link:** *Verify on Karnataka RERA Portal (rera.karnataka.gov.in)*

Making the registration number an active outbound link to the government verification page signals total confidence.

### 2. Standardized RERA Carpet Area Tables
Under RERA, selling property on ambiguous "super built-up area" is illegal. Homebuyers want to know the exact net usable floor area inside the walls.

Provide transparent specification tables:
- Unit Configuration (e.g., 3BHK Type A)
- RERA Carpet Area (e.g., 1,180 sq ft)
- Exclusive Balcony & Utility Area (e.g., 145 sq ft)
- Common Area Proportion (e.g., 285 sq ft)
- Total Super Built-up Area for reference (e.g., 1,610 sq ft)

### 3. Downloadable Sanctioned Plans and Approvals
Host clean, readable PDF downloads of your statutory certificates:
- Commencement Certificate (CC)
- Fire Department NOC
- Airport Authority Height Clearance
- Pollution Control Board Environmental Clearance

When prospective buyers and their legal advocates can download approvals with one click, legal due diligence shrinks from three weeks to forty-eight hours.

### 4. Timestamped Quarterly Construction Updates
Under RERA, developers are required to upload quarterly progress reports. Put these on your public website:
- High-resolution photographs of current slab casting and brickwork
- Tower-by-tower completion percentages
- Actual completion date vs RERA committed deadline

Prospective buyers visiting the site see active work, proving that cash flow is healthy and delivery is on schedule.

### 5. Clear Bank APF Numbers for Loan Approvals
Homebuyers want to know which major financial institutions have approved the project:
- State Bank of India (SBI) APF Code
- HDFC / ICICI Bank Project Approval Codes

Displaying approved banking partners proves that premier national banks have already vetted the title deeds and found them marketable.
