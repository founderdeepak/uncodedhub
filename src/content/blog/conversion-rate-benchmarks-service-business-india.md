## 2. The 3 Primary Conversion Killers on Indian Websites

When a service business experiences an abysmal 0.4% conversion rate, it is almost always caused by three structural friction points:

### Friction Point 1: The WhatsApp Disconnect
In India, over 70% of high-intent buyers prefer messaging over filling out long web forms. Websites that lack an instantaneous, floating WhatsApp click-to-chat button lose more than half of their potential mobile inquiries immediately.

### Friction Point 2: The Missing Price Anchor
When websites completely refuse to mention pricing thresholds, visitors assume the service is either exorbitantly overpriced or intentionally deceptive. Publishing clear *"Starting At"* tiers qualifies buyers and boosts inquiry confidence.

### Friction Point 3: The 4-Second Mobile Latency Cliff
As documented in Google Core Web Vitals research, for every single second of page load latency past 2 seconds, conversion rates drop by an average of 18%. A slow, bloated WordPress site loading in 5.5 seconds has already lost over half its prospective buyers before the first image renders.
