## Frequently asked questions

**Why do surgical case volume logbooks provide unmatched social proof?**  
Surgical success is directly correlated with high procedure volume. Transparently documenting cumulative case counts (e.g., 'Over 2,500 Successful Joint Replacements') validates clinical mastery.

**What details should be included in a surgical volume showcase?**  
Break down cases by specific sub-procedures, highlight low complication and infection rates, and update volume metrics annually to reflect active surgical practice.

**Does high case volume reassure high-risk surgical candidates?**  
Yes. Patients with complex co-morbidities specifically search for high-volume surgeons who have navigated every anatomical variation and complication.
