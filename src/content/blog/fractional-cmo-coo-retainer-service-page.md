The traditional model of hiring permanent, full-time C-suite leadership is rapidly breaking down for high-growth Series-A and Series-B companies.

Hiring a full-time Chief Operating Officer (COO), Chief Marketing Officer (CMO), or Chief Technology Officer (CTO) in India's tech hubs demands an annual compensation package between ₹1.2 Crores and ₹2.5 Crores, plus significant equity grants, recruiter search fees, and a four-to-six-month hiring cycle.

For many companies, paying that full-time package is unnecessary and financially reckless. What they actually need is **10 to 15 hours per week of veteran strategic oversight** to unblock bottlenecks, establish systems, and mentor junior department heads.

This shift has created the rise of the **Fractional Executive**.

Retainer fees for seasoned fractional leaders range from ₹2,00,000 to ₹8,00,000 per month per client.

However, selling fractional leadership is tricky: clients fear you will act like an unaccountable external freelancer who disappears when crises strike. 

Your service page must project institutional discipline, clear operational scopes, and rigorous Service Level Agreements (SLAs).

---

## 1. Deconstructing the Fractional Executive Service Architecture

A high-converting fractional leadership page defines the relationship across three dimensions:

```
[Fractional Leadership Service Architecture]
├── The Strategic Scope: High-level systems, board alignment & KPI ownership
├── The Weekly Operational Cadence: Dedicated days, executive meetings & slack access
├── What Is Explicitly Excluded: Routine tactical task execution (delegated to internal staff)
└── The Transition Horizon: Building systems so internal leaders can take over in 6-12 months
```

Explicitly stating that your ultimate objective is to **build internal capabilities and work yourself out of the job** reassures founders that you are a genuine strategic partner, not a permanent parasite on their payroll.

## 3. The 90-Day Operational Roadmap

Founders evaluate fractional leaders based on how fast they can establish order. Present a standardized **First 90-Day Playbook**:
- **Days 1–30 (Audit & Diagnostics):** Deep-dive interviews with all direct reports, P&L audit, identification of top 3 operational leaks.
- **Days 31–60 (System Architecture):** Deployment of standardized scorecards, sprint planning rhythms, and automated reporting dashboards.
- **Days 61–90 (Execution & Mentorship):** Mentoring internal team leads, establishing hiring criteria for permanent replacements, board-ready KPI alignment.

## Fractional Service Page Checklist

- [ ] Clear distinction between strategic fractional leadership and routine task freelancing
- [ ] Standardized 3-tier retainer packages with transparent weekly time allocations
- [ ] 90-day operational onboarding roadmap establishing immediate ROI milestones
- [ ] Explicit communication SLAs (e.g., Slack response windows, board meeting attendance)
- [ ] Verifiable founder testimonials citing specific operational metrics improved
- [ ] Diagnostic application form capturing company ARR, team size, and immediate bottleneck
