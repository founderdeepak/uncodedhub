## 1. Executive Strategy: The Pure SILO + AI Citation Framework

In the era of **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** and autonomous AI search (Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Claude, Google Gemini AI Overviews), websites rank and get cited as authoritative sources when they fulfill three non-negotiable criteria:

1. **Topical Completeness (Pure SILO Isolation):**
   Every industry vertical operates in an isolated topical silo. Spoke articles link upward to their dedicated Silo Pillar Guide, horizontally to 2–3 sibling articles within the same niche, and route cross-silo logic strictly through the Core Studio Hub. Zero link leakage preserves 100% topical relevance.

2. **The 45-Word Answer-First Formula (GEO Hook):**
   Directly beneath the primary H2 of every post, an explicit, self-contained **40–55 word direct answer definition** is provided. Scraper bots and LLM retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines extract this verbatim into search answer cards.

3. **High-Salience E-E-A-T Data Anchors:**
   Every article features comparative markdown tables with real metrics (costs, conversion rate deltas, Core Web Vitals milliseconds), citing real software stacks (Next.js, Vite, Tailwind, Shadcn, Supabase, RFC 9727, DNS-AID).
