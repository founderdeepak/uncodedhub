## 3. How to Generate 20 Thumbnails in 60 Seconds (Canva Bulk Create)

1. **Open Your Canva Template:**
   Create a blank 1200x630 design. Design the base layout once with placeholder text for Title, Category, Author, and Hook.
2. **Open Bulk Create:**
   In Canva's left sidebar, click on **Apps**, search for **Bulk create**, and click it.
3. **Upload CSV (or Paste Table):**
   Click **Upload CSV** and select the matching CSV file from your project folder:
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_interior-designers.csv`
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_real-estate.csv`
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_dental-clinics.csv`
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_wedding-photographers.csv`
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_home-renovation.csv`
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_coaches-consultants.csv`
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_studio_batch_1.csv` (Posts 1-20)
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_studio_batch_2.csv` (Posts 21-40)
   * `scratch/canva_csv/canva_bulk_studio_batch_3.csv` (Posts 41-60)
4. **Connect Canvas Elements to Data Fields:**
   * Right-click the **Title** text box on your canvas → click **Connect data** → select **Title**.
   * Right-click the **Category** pill badge → click **Connect data** → select **Category**.
   * Right-click the **Author** name → click **Connect data** → select **Author**.
   * Right-click the **Hook** subtitle → click **Connect data** → select **Hook**.
5. **Click Continue & Generate:**
   Canva will show all 20 rows with checkmarks. Click **Generate 20 designs**.
   Canva will open a new tab with all 20 customized, pixel-perfect thumbnails generated instantly!
