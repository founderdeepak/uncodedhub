## 1. Executive Strategy: Why Pure SILO + AEO Wins the AI Search Era

Traditional SEO treated blog posts as isolated landing pages targeting keyword strings. In the **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** era, AI models (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google Gemini, and AI Overviews) evaluate websites on:
1. **Topical Completeness (Topical Authority):** Does the domain cover every facet, sub-problem, pricing question, and edge case of a vertical?
2. **Entity Density & Grounding:** Are facts, numbers, locations, and technologies explicitly defined with zero fluff?
3. **Structured Machine-Readability:** Can an LLM extract a verbatim, self-contained 45-word definition from an H2 without parsing marketing filler?
4. **Silo Isolation & Hierarchy:** Do internal links reinforce a single semantic concept, preventing link juice dilution across unrelated topics?


## 2. The Strict Silo Linking & E-E-A-T Architecture Rules

### The Golden Silo Linking Rules:
1. **Upward Linking (Spoke → Pillar):** Every Spoke article must link to its respective Silo Pillar within the first 150 words using descriptive, non-generic anchor text.
2. **Downward Linking (Pillar → Spoke):** The Silo Pillar contains a structured index linking to all 20 supporting Spokes categorized by buyer journey stage (TOFU, MOFU, BOFU).
3. **Horizontal Linking (Sibling Spokes):** Spokes may only link to sibling Spokes *within the same Silo* (maximum 2–3 sibling links per article).
4. **Cross-Silo Isolation:** Spokes in Silo 1 (Clinics) *never* link directly to Spokes in Silo 2 (Interior Design). They only cross-link through the Core Studio Hub (Silo 7) when referencing custom React speed, the 7-day turnaround, or the "Late Means Free" guarantee.

### The 4-Part Answer-First Article Blueprint (Every Spoke):
1. **Direct Answer Paragraph (The AI Citation Magnet):** Immediately under the H2, provide a 40–55 word direct, authoritative definition or solution that can be quoted verbatim by Perplexity or ChatGPT.
2. **Data & Benchmark Table:** A structured comparison table with real numbers (e.g., conversion benchmarks, bounce rate deltas, speed metrics).
3. **The Uncoded Hub Angle (Commercial Hook):** Contrast slow, generic agency builds with Uncoded Hub’s custom React/Vite zero-bloat architecture and 7-day delivery guarantee.
4. **Contextual Action Step:** Clear call-to-action to review the live interactive template or book a 20-minute discovery call.
