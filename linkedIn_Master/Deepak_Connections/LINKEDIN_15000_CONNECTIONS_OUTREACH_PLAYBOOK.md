## 🛡️ LinkedIn Safe Sending Protocol (Anti-Ban Rules)

Because these are **1st-degree connections**, you do not need InMail credits and messages land directly in their primary inbox. However, LinkedIn's spam algorithm monitors messaging frequency. Follow these strict rules:

1. **Daily Volume:** Send **50 to 70 DMs per day maximum**.
2. **Spacing:** Leave a 30–60 second gap between sends (if sending manually, this happens naturally).
3. **Pacing:**
   - Week 1: 30–40 DMs / day (Warm-up)
   - Week 2+: 50–70 DMs / day (Steady state)
4. **Time of Day:** Best response rates occur between **9:30 AM – 11:30 AM** and **5:00 PM – 7:30 PM** in their local timezone.
5. **Anti-Spam Dynamic Variance:** The generator automatically alternates between 3 distinct opening hooks, sign-offs, and sentence variations across the database so your messages do not look like automated identical clones.
