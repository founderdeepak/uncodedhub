### DAY 2
#### Post 3 (09:00 AM) — The 3 Typographic Mistakes That Make Websites Look Cheap
* **Theme**: Typographic Discipline
* **Focus**: Font Pairing & Visual Hierarchy

In my years designing executive presentations, typography was 80% of the game.
You can have a great layout, but if your typography is broken, the entire design looks amateur.

Here are the 3 most common typographic mistakes I see on service firm websites:

1. The "Too Many Families" Syndrome:
Using 3 or 4 different font families on a single page.
Rule: Never use more than 2 font families. One high-character editorial serif or modern geometric sans-serif for headings, and one neutral, highly legible sans-serif for body text.

2. Line-Height Suffocation:
Body text lines squished together with no vertical breathing room.
When line-height is under 1.5, reading on mobile becomes exhausting. Your visitors’ eyes get tired and they bounce.

3. Lack of Scale Hierarchy:
When your subheadings are almost the same size as your body text, the eye doesn't know where to land.
A high-converting page requires dramatic, intentional contrast in type scale: H1 -> H2 -> H3 -> Body.

Typography is not about decoration.
Typography is the voice your brand speaks in before the reader hears a word.
