## 1. THE SILO ARCHITECTURE BLUEPRINT

A proper SEO Silo isolates thematic clusters into distinct, hyper-focused content silos. This ensures search engine crawlers (Googlebot, Bingbot) and AI indexing engines (GPTBot, PerplexityBot) recognize Uncoded Hub as the definitive topical authority for **niche-specific business web design**.

```mermaid
graph TD
    Home["uncodedhub.com (Homepage: Brand & Global Authority)"] --> Services["/services (Primary Commercial Hub)"]
    Home --> Portfolio["/portfolio (Visual Proof & Deliverables)"]
    Home --> BlogHub["/blog (Journal & Knowledge Hub)"]
    
    BlogHub --> Silo1["SILO 1: Interior Designers & Architects"]
    BlogHub --> Silo2["SILO 2: Real Estate & Property Consultants"]
    BlogHub --> Silo3["SILO 3: Dental & Aesthetic Clinics"]
    BlogHub --> Silo4["SILO 4: Wedding Photographers & Events"]
    BlogHub --> Silo5["SILO 5: Home Renovation & Modular Kitchens"]
    BlogHub --> Silo6["SILO 6: Coaches & Consultants"]
    BlogHub --> Silo7["SILO 7: Studio & Web Engineering Benchmarks"]

    Silo1 --> S1_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 1)"]
    Silo2 --> S2_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 2)"]
    Silo3 --> S3_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 3)"]
    Silo4 --> S4_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 4)"]
    Silo5 --> S5_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 5)"]
    Silo6 --> S6_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 6)"]
    Silo7 --> S7_Articles["6-10 Cluster Articles (Inter-linked within Silo 7)"]

    S1_Articles -.->|Upward Link| Silo1
    S1_Articles -.->|Conversion CTA| Services
```

### The 4 Unbreakable Silo Rules:
1. **Vertical Linking Only (Up & Down):** Every cluster post links *up* to its Silo Hub / Pillar page, and the Pillar page links *down* to every cluster post.
2. **Lateral Linking Within the Same Silo Only:** An Interior Design blog post may link to another Interior Design blog post, but **NEVER** directly to a Dental Clinic blog post. Cross-linking between unrelated silos leaks topical relevance and dilutes crawl equity.
3. **Upward Commercial Bridges:** Every cluster post carries exactly one context-matched conversion bridge to the main transactional pages (`/services`, `/portfolio`, or `/contact`).
4. **Zero Cannibalization Rule:** Every single URL on the website has exactly **one** designated primary target keyword. No two URLs ever target the same primary query.
