### *Pre-Publishing Quality Assurance & Content Blueprint Matrix*

Use this ledger to draft, review, and track the exact production fields of every BOFU page before going live.

---

## 1. Quality Standards
* **H1:** Always in **Title Case**. Must contain target keyword.
* **H2s:** Always in **Sentence case**. Informative, engaging, solving real pain points.
* **Page Title:** Max 60 characters / 580px. Format: `Target Keyword | Benefit Hook | Brand`.
* **Meta Description:** Max 155 characters. Target keyword in the opening sentence.
* **OG Title / OG Description:** Optimized for clean visual cards on WhatsApp, X (Twitter), and LinkedIn.
