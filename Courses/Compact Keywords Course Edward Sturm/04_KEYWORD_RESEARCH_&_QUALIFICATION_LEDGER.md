# 🔍 Keyword Research & Qualification Ledger
### *Edward Sturm's Compact Keyword Research & Scoring System*

Use this ledger to record, filter, and prioritize compact keywords before drafting pages.

---

## 1. Column Definitions & Formulae

* **Keyword:** The exact search query typed by the user.
* **Category 1 / Category 2:** Business division or industry silo (e.g. `Healthcare`, `Design Studios`, `B2B`).
* **Avg Volume:** Monthly search volume (from Moz / Ahrefs / Google Keyword Planner).
* **Min / Max Volume:** Search volume range (e.g., 11 to 50, 51 to 100).
* **Difficulty (0–100):** Keyword difficulty score. We actively seek **Difficulty < 25**.
* **SERP CTR (%):** Percentage of searchers who actually click an organic result rather than zero-click SERP features.
* **Moz Priority:** Moz calculated priority combining volume, difficulty, and CTR.
* **Site Relevance (1–5):** How directly this keyword represents someone who needs your exact service (5 = instant fit).
* **Notes:** Specific observations on SERP results (e.g., "Top results are forum threads and old 2018 blogs; wide open for a dedicated BOFU page").
* **Targeted? (Yes / No):** Final go / no-go decision for this batch.

## 3. Deprioritization Rules (When to Say NO)
1. **Lacks Buyer Intent:** Keywords starting with "how to", "what is", or "best [x] list" belong to TOFU / affiliates. Deprioritize.
2. **High Domain Authority Dominance:** If all top 3 positions are owned by Wikipedia, Government sites, or DA 85+ portals without room to satisfy search intent, deprioritize.
3. **Mismatched Intent:** If the user is looking to download free code templates rather than hire a professional studio, discard immediately.
