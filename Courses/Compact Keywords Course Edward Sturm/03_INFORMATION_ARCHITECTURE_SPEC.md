# 🏛️ Information Architecture (IA) & Internal Linking Specification
### *Maximizing Crawl Efficiency & PageRank Distribution for Compact Keywords*

---

## 1. The 3 Core Rules of BOFU Architecture

1. **The 2-Click Rule:**
   Any prospective client or search engine spider must be able to reach any bottom-of-funnel landing page in **2 clicks or fewer** from the homepage (`/`).
2. **Zero Orphan Pages:**
   Google ignores pages that do not have meaningful internal links pointing to them. Every landing page must live inside an authoritative category hub.
3. **The Universal Footer Anchor:**
   The website footer is rendered on **every single URL** of the domain. Placing links to top-tier BOFU hubs or high-value landing pages in the footer instantly transmits sitewide link equity (PageRank) to those pages.

## 4. Internal Inlinks Calculation & Breadcrumb Schema

Every BOFU landing page must have:
* **Breadcrumb Navigation:** `Home > Services > [Page Title]`
* **Contextual Inlinks:** At least 2 links from relevant blog articles pointing to the BOFU page with natural partial-match anchor text.
* **Reciprocal Outlinks:** The BOFU page should link back to the main `/services/` hub and link out to 1 high-authority external benchmark (e.g., Google PageSpeed Insights, W3C standard).
