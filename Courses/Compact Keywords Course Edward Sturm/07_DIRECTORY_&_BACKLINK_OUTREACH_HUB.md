### The 4 Daily Channels
1. **Featured.com** (Formerly Terkel) — The premier platform for founder quotes.
2. **SourceOfSources.com (SOS)** — Daily email digest of active reporter requests.
3. **App.Qwoted.com** — Direct messaging with verified journalists.
4. **HelpAB2BWriter.com** — B2B specific writing queries.

```text
Hi [Reporter Name],

[Sentence 1 - Direct Answer]: In our experience engineering websites for private clinics and architecture studios, the #1 factor driving conversions isn't visual fluff—it's sub-second page speed that builds immediate unconscious trust.

[Sentence 2 - Concrete Data/Proof]: Across 40+ client deployments, reducing load time from 4.2 seconds to 0.7 seconds increased WhatsApp discovery call bookings by 180%.

[Sentence 3 - Actionable Takeaway]: Business owners should audit their third-party JavaScript plugins before running ad campaigns, as unoptimized scripts silently drain paid ad budgets.

Best,

Founders, Uncoded Hub (https://uncodedhub.com)
```
