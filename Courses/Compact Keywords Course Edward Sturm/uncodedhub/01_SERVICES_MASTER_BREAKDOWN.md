* **Pricing:** ₹55,000 – ₹90,000 (India) / $1,500 – $3,000 (Global).

* **Pricing:** ₹25,000 – ₹35,000 (India) / $650 – $1,000 (Global).

### Service 4: Defensive Compact SEO Retainer (Post-Launch Engine)
