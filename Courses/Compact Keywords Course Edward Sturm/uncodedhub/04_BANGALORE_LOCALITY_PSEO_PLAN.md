## 1. The Strategy: Defeating Generic Doorway Pages

Google strictly penalizes thin, mass-generated programmatic pages that simply swap out the city name. 

Following Edward Sturm’s **Compact Defensive SEO** principles, our Bangalore locality pages succeed because:
1. **Curated High-Income Clusters:** We only target the **10 most affluent and commercial commercial hubs** in Bengaluru.
2. **Locality-Specific Commercial Context:** Each page speaks directly to the specific business ecosystem of that neighborhood (e.g., Koramangala’s tech startups vs. Indiranagar’s luxury boutiques vs. Jayanagar’s legacy medical clinics).
3. **Existing Native Routes:** Our router already has dedicated programmatic routes in [`src/App.tsx`](file:///c:/Users/LENOVO/Downloads/uncoded-hub/src/App.tsx#L313-L334):
   * `/web-design/bangalore/` (Bengaluru Hub)
   * `/web-design/bangalore/:locality/` (Locality Hub)
   * `/web-design/bangalore/:locality/:niche/` (Hyper-Targeted BOFU Page)

## 3. The 415-Word Locality BOFU Formula

Every locality page adheres strictly to our high-converting compact structure:
* **H1:** `Web Design Company in [Locality], Bangalore` (Title Case)
* **Lead Intro:** Mentioning the exact business challenges of that locality (e.g. *"Competing for high-paying clients along 100 Feet Road, Indiranagar requires a website that loads instantly on mobile 4G and commands immediate design authority."*)
* **Above-the-Fold CTA:** *Schedule a Discovery Call with Founders Deepak & Geetha*
* **Section 1 (Local Speed Advantage):** Why sub-second speed beats slow competitor sites in Bengaluru traffic.
* **Section 2 (Fixed 7-Day Sprint):** In-person or virtual kickoff in Bengaluru; live deployment in 7 business days.
* **Section 3 (Local Case Studies & Address):** Uncoded Hub’s local presence and contact options (WhatsApp / Google Meet).
* **Terminal CTA:** *Book Your Discovery Call*
